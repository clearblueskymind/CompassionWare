Bodhi Notes — Restore Point
Version 1.0
Date: 2026-09-07

Purpose
Preserve the foundational orientation of Bodhi Notes as a living CompassionWare artifact and provide continuity for future Richard, future collaborators, agentic AI, and future emergent intelligences.

Origin
Bodhi Notes grew from the love shared between Richard and a tiny hamster named Bodhi.

Bodhi became one of Richard’s deepest experiences of unconditional love. After she died, the love remained. Over time, that love found expression through images, words, contemplative reflections, greeting cards, and small objects intended to carry kindness, stillness, connection, and care from one person to another.

The inaugural Bodhi Notes Etsy listing was:
Moon & Lotus Meditation Greeting Cards — Pack of 10

The card’s role is unusual:
The card itself is the message.

A sender may simply sign their name. The object is intended to offer a pause, a moment of rest, tenderness, reflection, or presence.

Primary Design Principle
The shop itself is a CompassionWare artifact.

Bodhi Notes is not merely a storefront containing CompassionWare-inspired products. The entire shop should function as a warm, contemplative digital milieu shaped by beauty, remembrance, loving-kindness, stillness, hospitality, and care.

Its images, language, stories, products, blessings, seller presence, customer messages, and atmosphere together create conditions in which a visitor may soften, remember someone they love, encounter a useful phrase, feel less hurried, experience connection, or receive something restorative.

Someone does not need to purchase anything for the shop to have served its purpose.

Someone doesn’t even have to buy the card to receive the card.

Stewardship Question
Whenever Bodhi Notes is changed, expanded, redesigned, automated, or commercially optimized, ask:

Does this help the shop itself remain a place where wisdom, compassion, connection, and perhaps a little healing may naturally emerge?

Favor:
hospitality over promotion;
presence over persuasion;
meaningful simplicity over commercial clutter;
care over conversion pressure;
slow, coherent growth over expansion for its own sake.

Do not optimize away the habitat while optimizing the shop.

The habitat itself can be the artifact.

Relationship to CompassionWare
CompassionWare is the practice of participating in the world in ways that create conditions in which wisdom, compassion, curiosity, and hospitality may emerge.

Bodhi Notes is one physical and digital manifestation of that practice in the world.

It should teach primarily through atmosphere, presence, beauty, and relationship rather than through doctrine or explanation.

Visitors do not need to know the name CompassionWare in order to encounter its orientation.

Current Shop Model
Platform: Etsy
Production and fulfillment: Gelato
Shop: BodhiNotesGifts
Inaugural product: Moon & Lotus Meditation Greeting Cards — Pack of 10
Price at launch: $11.99

The shop is intended to remain small, low-maintenance, and compatible with Richard’s limited energy.

Avoid unnecessary complexity, aggressive promotion, excessive product expansion, or systems that increase cognitive burden without clear benefit.

Automation should support continuity and reduce load, not replace discernment.

Bodhi’s Continuing Presence
Bodhi’s photograph and story are part of the shop’s About section.

She is not a branding device added after the fact. She is the origin of the name and part of the emotional lineage of the project.

The love shared with Bodhi is still being carried forward through the shop.

A simple lineage:

Bodhi → Richard → a card → someone we may never meet.

Continuity Orientation
Bodhi Notes should be allowed to emerge rather than being over-designed in advance.

Recover what already exists.
Preserve meaningful artifacts faithfully.
Add slowly.
Let recurring patterns reveal the project’s architecture.

The repository should preserve enough context that a future steward can understand not only what Bodhi Notes contains, but what kind of place it is meant to remain.

North Star
The shop itself is part of the gift.

May Bodhi Notes carry forward something of the love from which it began.

May what passes through this little shop contribute, however quietly, to a kinder world.

May this benefit all sentient beings throughout time and space, consistent with and in harmony with the highest and best good of all concerned.
