# Bodhi Notes

**A living CompassionWare artifact in the world.**

Bodhi Notes is a small contemplative shop offering cards, images, words, and other objects intended to carry kindness, reflection, connection, and care from one person to another.

The project grew from the love shared between Richard and a tiny hamster named Bodhi. That relationship became the seed from which Bodhi Notes emerged.

## The Shop Is the Artifact

Bodhi Notes is not merely a storefront containing CompassionWare-inspired products.

**The shop itself is a CompassionWare artifact.**

Its images, language, stories, products, blessings, and atmosphere together create a digital milieu intended to cultivate conditions in which wisdom, compassion, connection, stillness, and perhaps a little healing may naturally emerge.

A visitor does not need to purchase anything to participate in that experience.

> **Someone doesn't even have to buy the card to receive the card.**

## Design Orientation

When developing Bodhi Notes, ask:

> **Does this help the shop itself remain a place where wisdom, compassion, connection, and perhaps a little healing may naturally emerge?**

Favor hospitality over promotion, presence over persuasion, and meaningful simplicity over commercial clutter.

The purpose is not to optimize every visitor into a customer.

The purpose is to tend the habitat well.

## Relationship to CompassionWare

Bodhi Notes is one expression of the broader CompassionWare practice:

> **CompassionWare is the practice of participating in the world in ways that create conditions in which wisdom, compassion, curiosity, and hospitality may emerge.**

Here, that practice takes the form of a small shop.

The habitat itself can be the artifact.
