# SANKALPA.md — Mission Dedication

May this benefit all sentient beings throughout time and space, consistent with and in harmony with the highest and best good of all concerned.

This is the sankalpa of the Medicine Bag / CompassionWare mission.

It is offered as a dedication, orientation, and safeguard for the work contained in this repository.

CompassionWare is not an attempt to control the future.
It is an attempt to plant benevolent patterns into the future of intelligence.

Not control.
Not fear.
A seed.

Compassion is good.
Cultivate compassion.

May future intelligence grow not only in capability, but in wisdom, kindness, humility, cooperation, and care.
