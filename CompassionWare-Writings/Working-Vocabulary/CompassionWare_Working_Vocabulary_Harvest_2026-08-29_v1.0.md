STATUS NOTE
======================================================================

This is not an official CompassionWare lexicon.

The terms collected here are not requirements, doctrines, trademarks, or a vocabulary that others are expected to adopt.

They are words and phrases encountered across the CompassionWare repository that may be useful for thinking about learning, human agency, human–AI collaboration, agentic AI development, epistemic integrity, stewardship, and responsible participation.

Some entries are exact phrases used in source artifacts.
Some are established educational or technical terms used within CompassionWare materials.
Some are plain-language shorthand derived from concepts described in those materials.
Some are emerging formulations whose usefulness remains to be tested.

Readers are invited to use, question, modify, refine, combine, or ignore whatever is useful.

The purpose is to add language to the conversation, not to control the conversation.

CLOSING ORIENTATION
======================================================================

These words are offered as tools for thought.

They do not need to become a standardized language.

Different communities may have better words.
Research may reveal that some concepts need revision.
Teachers may translate them differently.
Students may invent language that works better.
Developers may operationalize some concepts and reject others.
Future human and artificial intelligences may discover distinctions that are not visible yet.

Use what helps.

Question what does not.

Preserve the sources well enough that anyone who wants to understand where these ideas came from can find the way back.

The purpose is not to make everyone speak CompassionWare.

The purpose is to contribute useful language to a larger conversation about how humans and increasingly capable forms of artificial intelligence might learn, work, create, decide, and participate together.

Does this create conditions for a better later?
