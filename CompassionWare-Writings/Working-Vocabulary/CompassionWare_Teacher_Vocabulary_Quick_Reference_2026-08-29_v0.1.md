# AI + Learning
## A Quick Vocabulary Reference for Teachers

*You do not need to master AI overnight.*  
*Your presence, judgment, and care still matter.*  
*You can learn alongside your students, one thoughtful step at a time.*

*A few useful ideas for teaching and learning alongside AI.*

---

## 🌱 Learner State

**What can this learner understand, retrieve, apply, and carry forward right now?**

Notice the learner — not just the assignment.

---

## 🎚️ Appropriate Complexity

**The right amount of challenge for this learner, in this moment.**

Not too easy. Not overwhelming.

---

## ⚓ Anchoring Before Advancing

**Sometimes learning needs to become stable before more is added.**

Consolidation can be progress.

---

## 🧗 Productive Struggle

**Some difficulty is part of learning when support is available.**

AI doesn't need to remove every challenge.

---

## 🤲 Calibrated Assistance

**Give enough help to support learning without doing the learning for the student.**

Support participation, not substitution.

---

## 👀 Tracking Before Teaching

**Notice what is actually happening before deciding what should happen next.**

Observe first. Intervene second.

---

## 🔎 Verification

**Check important AI-generated claims instead of accepting them automatically.**

AI output is something to examine, not simply trust.

---

## 🎒 Teach What Can Be Carried Away

**Favor learning the student can eventually use without the teacher or AI present.**

The learning should increasingly belong to the learner.

---
