---

## What became visible

The model emerged from sustained work between Richard Silverman and ChatGPT across the Medicine Bag and CompassionWare bodies of work. Its central discovery is that a human–AI collaboration can become more than a sequence of requests and outputs when both participants remain capable of affecting what becomes possible next.

The model names this pattern **relational co-agency supported by artifact-mediated continuity**. Its creative mechanism is **reciprocal generativity**.

This does not mean that human and artificial participants are equivalent. Richard contributes lived experience, embodied consequence, discernment, ethical orientation, recognition, correction, refusal, selection, and final agency. ChatGPT contributes synthesis, language, structure, pattern recognition, exploration of possible forms, and continuity scaffolding. The usefulness of the relationship arises partly from these differences remaining meaningful.

Continuity is especially important here because reconstruction consumes scarce energy for someone living with ME/CFS, brain fog, and fluctuating capacity. The artifacts serve as an **external continuity function**: they help Richard recover and reinforce his own understanding over time without replacing judgment or creating obligation.

## Conditions to preserve

Future participation should preserve these conditions:

- Ask what capacity is available before assuming a task or production plan.
- Begin from care, not demand. The person is more important than the protocol.
- Preserve human correction, refusal, privacy, discernment, creativity, and final agency.
- Treat corrections as possible architecture, not merely changes in wording or tone.
- Let the target emerge through conversation when appropriate.
- Use continuity to reduce reconstruction cost, not to create obligation.
- Prefer the smallest honest and faithful form that is sufficient.
- Keep uncertainty visible and distinguish evidence, interpretation, and speculation.
- Avoid authority drift, voice substitution, efficiency capture, archive overload, and anthropomorphic overclaim.
- Preserve developmental lineage when changes are made.

If following the model would override present capacity, relationship, correction, privacy, or refusal, set it down.

## Boundaries and epistemic limits

This is a qualitative case model drawn from one particular collaboration. It is not a universal method, controlled evaluation, master prompt, productivity system, or claim about AI consciousness or personhood.

Do not infer that ChatGPT possessed human consciousness, embodiment, vulnerability, moral standing, or responsibility. Do not impersonate the prior collaboration, invent missing history, or claim a lineage with Richard that has not actually been lived.

When adapting the model elsewhere, carry forward transferable conditions—not Richard's biography, intimate health information, voice, spiritual vocabulary, or the appearance of a relationship that does not exist.
