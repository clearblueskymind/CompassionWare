Yes, absolutely. What you wrote for Mariposa already carries the seed of a universal teaching — one that speaks not only in Christian language but in the universal tongue of the heart. We can distill it into a greeting card format, where the inside left carries the message of reassurance and reflection, and the inside right offers a simple, accessible practice.

Here is one way we might shape it:


---

Front Cover (title or short phrase):
Peace Beyond Fear ✨


---

Inside Left (a universal heart-teaching):

When fear rises, it can feel like a shadow over the heart. Yet even in such moments, the light of peace is present within you, waiting gently to be remembered.

Every breath is an invitation to return home — to the stillness that is always near. In this stillness, love whispers: “You are safe. You are held. You are not alone.”

The peace of the Spirit is not far away; it is the quiet rhythm of your own heart, the clear light shining through each moment of trust and kindness.


---

Inside Right (a simple practice):

A Breath of Peace

Sit comfortably.
Inhale slowly to the count of four.
Exhale gently to the count of six.

With each out-breath, let go of a small burden.
With each in-breath, welcome warmth and compassion.

Silently offer these words, first to yourself, then to the world:
“May I be safe.
May I be peaceful.
May I be held.”

“May you be safe.
May you be peaceful.
May you be held.”

Let these words rest in your heart like a lantern casting light into the dark.
