# 3. Relational Co-agency

**GitHub folder:**  
https://github.com/clearblueskymind/CompassionWare/tree/main/relational-co-agency

**Repository path:**  
`relational-co-agency/`

**Relationship to the Agentic Gardener:**  
Relational Co-agency is a separate companion body of work at the repository root. It should not be collapsed into the Agentic Gardener or moved beneath it merely because the projects are closely related.

It preserves a lived case model of Richard and ChatGPT's collaboration: reciprocal generativity, preserved human agency, capacity-sensitive participation, and continuity supported by artifacts. This model helps explain the relational conditions from which the Agentic Gardener emerged and the kind of human-AI relationship its design should protect.

It is contextual and ancestral orientation, not an Agentic Gardener operating constitution.

## 4.1 Authorization-Preserving Reorientation

**GitHub folder:**  
https://github.com/clearblueskymind/CompassionWare/tree/main/Authorization-Preserving-Reorientation

**Repository path:**  
`Authorization-Preserving-Reorientation/`

**Relationship to the Agentic Gardener:**  
Authorization-Preserving Reorientation, or APR, is an emerging and deliberately falsifiable agentic-AI control inquiry. It asks whether an agent can leave a locally advantageous but unauthorized strategy without losing the legitimate objective that brought it there.

This work is directly relevant to the Gardener's concern with stopping, interruptibility, authorization boundaries, resistance to task momentum, and preserving legitimate purpose during reorientation. It remains a separate research inquiry and should not be treated as a validated Gardener safeguard unless experimental evidence supports it.

**Primary doorway:**

**GitHub folder:**  
https://github.com/clearblueskymind/CompassionWare/tree/main/Mind%20Training%20Adaptive%20Tutor

**Repository path:**  
`Mind Training Adaptive Tutor/`

**Relationship to the Agentic Gardener:**  
The Mind Training Adaptive Tutor is an evolving CompassionWare experiment in AI-supported learning that seeks to increase awareness, freedom, skill, self-direction, and practical human capability.

It is a direct developmental source for the Agentic Gardener's tutor principle:

> Teach what can be carried away.

Its relevant inheritance includes adaptive pacing, demonstrated rather than presumed learning, orientation before intervention, attention to present capacity, learning that carries into ordinary life, and the principle that AI assistance should leave the human more capable rather than more dependent.

The Tutor remains a distinct educational habitat. It should not be reduced to an Agentic Gardener feature or treated as though its full curriculum governs the Gardener.

# Relationship Map

**CompassionWare** is the wider ethical and contemplative orientation.

**Relational Co-agency** preserves a lived model of conditions that support humane, generative human-AI participation while retaining meaningful difference and human final agency.

**The Agentic Gardener** develops those and other CompassionWare principles into character, governance, safety, permission, continuity, stewardship, and eventually bounded technical agency.

**Ask CompassionWare** is a narrower public concierge living within the Agentic Gardener habitat and inheriting its deeper orientation without claiming its full role or authority.

**Authorization-Preserving Reorientation** explores a potentially relevant but not yet validated control hypothesis concerning unauthorized strategy and legitimate objective preservation.

**Mind Training Adaptive Tutor** contributes a developmental lineage for capability-building, adaptive pacing, and learning without dependency.

**From the CompassionWare Garden** carries related questions into public culture through humor, imagery, and editorial invitation.

In compact form:

> CompassionWare provides the orientation.  
> Relational Co-agency illuminates the relational conditions.  
> The Agentic Gardener embodies the stewardship architecture.  
> Ask CompassionWare welcomes visitors at the door.
