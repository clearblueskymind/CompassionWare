## Purpose

Use one copy of this template for each selected Richard–ChatGPT conversation moment.

The purpose is to preserve enough primary evidence to examine contextual and relational humor without allowing later theory to overwrite what actually happened.

Preserve the ordinary lead-in as carefully as the humorous turn. The evidence is not merely the apparent punchline. It includes the relational conditions, accumulated references, timing, response, risks, repair, and aftermath.

## Cross-Case Reminder

Do not treat a single case as proof of emergence, causation, consciousness, alignment, safety, or relational understanding.

After completing the first four records, compare them for recurring conditions, differences, counterexamples, and competing explanations.

> **The laughter is part of the provenance, but it is not the conclusion.**

Preserve first. Interpret carefully. Keep the freedom to find no coherent pattern. 🌱😂
