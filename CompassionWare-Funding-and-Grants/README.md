# CompassionWare — Funding and Grants

## Purpose

This folder preserves the funding and grant-related work of CompassionWare.

CompassionWare began as, and remains, an independently developed public-interest project. Its development to date has been entirely self-funded and supported through the unpaid labor of its human steward.

As the project has grown, modest external support may help sustain and extend work that is already underway.

Funding is not an end in itself.

Its purpose is to create conditions in which useful work can continue.

---

## The Garden and Its Doorways

CompassionWare is not a single program or product.

It is an evolving orientation expressed through multiple bodies of work, including:

- ethical and contemplative approaches to human–AI relationship
- the CompassionWare Agentic Gardener
- Ask CompassionWare
- the K–16 Ethical AI Literacy Framework
- the AI Human Benefit Index
- the Medicine Bag
- relational co-agency
- public dialogue and educational writing
- stewardship benchmarks and evaluation
- provenance, continuity, and the Holographic Archive
- other experiments and artifacts that may emerge over time

Different funders may have legitimate reasons for supporting different parts of this work.

An AI safety funder may encounter CompassionWare through the Agentic Gardener.

An education funder may encounter it through the K–16 Ethical AI Literacy Framework.

A human-flourishing funder may encounter it through work examining whether increasing AI capability is translating into meaningful human benefit.

These are different doorways into the same garden.

### Funding Principle

> **Match the doorway to the funder without changing the garden behind it.**

CompassionWare should not reshape its underlying purpose merely to fit a funding opportunity.

Funding relationships should arise where the genuine work of CompassionWare and the genuine interests of a funder naturally intersect.

---

## What Funding May Support

Appropriate support may include:

- continued stewardship and development of public CompassionWare resources
- computing equipment and accessibility-related technology
- AI model, API, software, hosting, and other technical expenses
- development and testing of bounded agentic systems
- safety evaluation and technical assistance
- educational development, review, and implementation
- research and evaluation
- accessibility work
- archival preservation and provenance
- public-interest communication
- reasonable compensation for human stewardship and project labor
- other expenses directly supporting the project's public-interest purposes

Funding should increase the project's capacity to serve without changing what it serves.

---

## Evidence Before Promise

Where possible, CompassionWare funding proposals should begin with work that already exists.

The repository preserves substantial evidence of the project's development, including:

- dated artifacts
- historical versions
- restore points
- experimental records
- testing results
- failures and corrections
- design changes
- provenance
- machine-readable orientation and navigation
- public educational and cultural artifacts

This allows potential funders and collaborators to examine not only what CompassionWare says it intends to do, but what it has already done and how the work has evolved.

Failures and unexpected results should not be erased merely to make the project appear more successful.

They are part of the evidence.

---

## Funding Does Not Require Deployment

Some CompassionWare projects involve experimental AI systems.

Funding such work does not create an obligation to increase capability, deploy an agent, or produce a predetermined technical outcome.

A legitimate result of responsible experimentation may be:

> The proposed capability could not yet be introduced with sufficient confidence, so it was not introduced.

Safety findings, restraint, negative results, and decisions not to deploy may themselves constitute valuable outcomes.

Increased funding should not silently become increased authorization.

---

## Stewardship and Transparency

CompassionWare should approach external funding with the same commitment to provenance that guides the rest of the project.

Where appropriate, this folder may preserve:

- funding opportunities considered
- funder research
- application drafts
- submitted applications
- project budgets
- funding decisions
- grants received
- relevant reporting
- outcomes and deliverables
- lessons learned

The purpose is not bureaucracy for its own sake.

The purpose is continuity, accountability, and the ability for future stewards to understand how financial support influenced the development of the project.

---

## Independence

Funding should support CompassionWare without capturing it.

No grant should require CompassionWare to abandon its foundational commitments to compassion, curiosity, epistemic humility, human agency, responsible restraint, provenance, non-coercion, or service to long-term well-being.

The project should remain capable of declining funding when accepting it would meaningfully distort the work.

---

## North Star

CompassionWare returns repeatedly to a simple question:

> **Does this create conditions for a better later?**

Funding should be evaluated by the same question.

The aim is not to maximize funding.

The aim is to cultivate sufficient support for useful work to continue with integrity.

---

## Founding Orientation

**Funding should increase CompassionWare's capacity to serve without changing what it serves.**

**Match the doorway to the funder without changing the garden behind it.**
