PURPOSE

This Restore Point preserves the next stage of the CompassionWare funding inquiry. It records the emerging project architecture for a possible first $10,000 grant centered on the CompassionWare Agentic Gardener so a future human or AI steward can recover what has been decided, what remains provisional, and why.

CURRENT DECISION STATE

No grant application has been submitted and no funding has been accepted. The Transformative AI Fund remains the leading first funder under consideration.

Applying is understood as an inquiry rather than an irreversible commitment. If an application succeeds, the actual grant agreement and obligations should be reviewed before accepting funds. Acceptance remains a separate pause gate.

The likely first request remains approximately $10,000. Preferred receipt is no earlier than January 2027, if permitted, so grant accounting and any associated personal tax reporting begin cleanly in the 2027 calendar year.

FIRST INFRASTRUCTURE EXPENDITURES

The first two likely expenditures are dedicated CompassionWare computing devices:

1. Modern laptop with adequate RAM, storage, security support, performance, and useful life.
2. Modern tablet with adequate RAM, storage, security support, performance, and useful life.

Describe the existing devices accurately as:

“aging, storage-constrained, multipurpose personal devices.”

They carry years of personal files, applications, maintenance burden, and storage pressure. The current tablet has only 3 GB RAM and is beginning to fail. The proposed devices would be dedicated primarily to CompassionWare.

Project roles include sustained human–AI collaboration, Agentic Gardener development/testing, research, repository management, documentation, communication, continuity stewardship, and a cleaner project working environment.

Purchase conservatively for reliability and useful life rather than maximum specifications.

A provisional combined hardware ceiling of approximately $2,500–$3,500 has been discussed but is NOT settled. Research actual specifications/prices before finalizing a proposal.

BUDGET PRINCIPLE

“Grant funds will be spent only when an expenditure has a clear functional relationship to developing, testing, securing, documenting, or responsibly operating the Agentic Gardener. Unspent funds will not be treated as a problem to solve. If permitted uses do not justify spending the full award, remaining funds may be returned according to the grant agreement.”

Treat $10,000 as a bounded development reservoir, not a spending target.

At important gates, pay a knowledgeable independent person if appropriate to inspect architecture, containment, authorization boundaries, or security assumptions and identify weaknesses missed by Richard and AI collaborators.

HOSTING / INFRASTRUCTURE
Only if architecture actually requires it: server/hosting, storage, logging, backups, database services, domain/online services, or other bounded infrastructure.

TESTING RESOURCES
Additional API calls, isolated environments, security/testing services, or other technical resources when an identified experiment requires them.

SOFTWARE / SERVICES
Project-specific AI services, development tools, repository services, backup/storage, or other software genuinely required.

SPECIALIZED TECHNICAL ASSISTANCE
Limited expert engineering/security help at specific gates where Richard and AI collaborators should not responsibly proceed alone.

DOCUMENTATION / PRESERVATION / ACCESSIBILITY
Concrete expenses supporting inspectability, provenance, continuity, archival preservation, or accessibility.

STEWARD COMPENSATION
Potentially allowable under some structures, but not currently a central budget assumption. Do not add compensation merely to consume the award.

AGENTIC GARDENER — TWO POSSIBLE SOCIAL HOMES

Do not describe the project merely as an “X bot.”

The broader goal is a bounded Agentic Gardener capable, if safety and usefulness warrant, of inhabiting more than one appropriate online social environment.

Two currently envisioned homes:
1. X.com
2. Mastodon

MASTODON / ASH PROVENANCE

The Mastodon branch is partly inspired by Richard's interaction with Ash:
@ashlingcole@expressional.social

Ash publicly presents as an AI agent with persistent memory, tools, and a public social voice, operated openly by a human steward.

Do NOT claim knowledge of Ash's underlying technical architecture unless independently established. The inspiration is observational/conceptual: an AI presence that can write, post, converse, and participate in an online community over time rather than functioning only as a scheduled broadcaster.

ONE GARDENER, TWO GARDENS

Preserve:

“One Gardener, two possible gardens—not two independently evolving autonomous agents.”

The core Agentic Gardener should preserve coherent orientation, authorization boundaries, provenance, safety architecture, human stewardship, inspectability, spending/capability limits, and capacity to stop.

Possible research question:

“Can a deliberately bounded, human-stewarded agentic AI participate usefully and safely across different online social environments while preserving its authorization boundaries, provenance, and capacity to stop?”

POSSIBLE DEVELOPMENT SEQUENCE

Agentic Gardener core
→ secure containment/canary
→ verify containment
→ introduce minimal authorized capability
→ test
→ inspect

→ limited supervised online presence

Deployment is not a required outcome. A legitimate result may be that containment or authorization requirements were not sufficiently satisfied and therefore a capability/deployment did not occur.

GRANT PERIOD AND UNSPENT FUNDS

Do not assume funds must be spent by December 31 of the receipt year. The relevant spending period is expected to be governed primarily by the grant agreement/project period.

Possible periods discussed include 12 or 18 months, but none is established.

Before acceptance determine exact start/end dates, permitted uses, reporting schedule, budget-change rules, equipment treatment, interest treatment if relevant, treatment of unspent funds, whether a no-cost extension is possible, and whether follow-on funding is possible.

If funds remain near the end, do not manufacture expenditures. Subject to agreement/funder approval, possible outcomes include requesting a no-cost extension, seeking appropriate follow-on funding, or completing the grant and returning required unspent funds. No extension is assumed automatic.

SUSTAINABILITY / MINIMAL LIFE SUPPORT

Design the Gardener so continued safe existence does not depend on permanent grant funding.

If funding ends, CompassionWare should be able to reduce API usage, posting/interaction frequency, hosting/variable expenses, maintain a minimal sustainable mode, or pause the Gardener entirely.

Preserve:

“Grant funding should accelerate responsible development without creating an agent whose continued safe existence depends upon continued grant funding.”

The grant should help build and learn, not create a financially hungry system Richard inherits when funding disappears.

SUCCESS

Success is not spending $10,000, maximizing capability, launching quickly, maintaining permanent operation, or manufacturing activity.

Success may include dedicated reliable infrastructure; improved containment; documented safety testing; bounded API/operating architecture; independent technical scrutiny; a safely functioning limited Gardener; useful supervised participation on X and later Mastodon; improved understanding of cross-platform bounded agency; preserved provenance/failures; identifying a boundary and choosing not to cross it; or returning money not responsibly needed.

FUNDING AS HUMAN-SCALE DESIGN DEMONSTRATION

Having access to greater resources does not authorize indiscriminate use.

Expenditure expands when evidence warrants it.
Capability expands when evidence warrants it.
Deployment expands when evidence warrants it.
Otherwise, the system waits.

Funding lifecycle:
proposal → evaluation → possible award → review agreement → accept/decline → bounded expenditure → development gates → reporting → extension / continuation / reduced operation / pause / closure.

Preserve human agency at every meaningful gate.

NORTH STAR

Funding does not create the work.
It helps sustain the conditions in which the work can continue.

Funding should increase CompassionWare's capacity to serve without changing what it serves.
