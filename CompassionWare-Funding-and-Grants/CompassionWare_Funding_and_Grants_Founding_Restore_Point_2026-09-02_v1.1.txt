# 1. WHY THIS FUNDING BRANCH EXISTS

CompassionWare already exists.

It was not invented to qualify for a grant. Its public repository,
writings, educational work, safety experiments, benchmarks, public
dialogue, visual culture, continuity architecture, and related bodies of
work predate this funding branch.

At the founding of this branch:

-   CompassionWare has **no external funding**.
-   The work has been developed through Richard Silverman's unpaid
    stewardship and personal resources.
-   Richard intends to continue CompassionWare for the rest of his life,
    or for as long as he is able, whether or not a grant is ever
    received.
-   Funding is therefore **support for an existing vocation and
    public-interest practice**, not the source of its mission.
-   A rejected grant does not mean the work failed.
-   An awarded grant does not gain authority to redefine what the work
    serves.

Founding principle:

> **Funding does not create the work. It helps sustain the conditions in
> which the work can continue.**

A second principle follows:

> **Funding should increase CompassionWare's capacity to serve without
> changing what it serves.**

# 2. THE GARDEN AND THE DOORWAYS

CompassionWare is not one program, one product, one grant category, or
one technical intervention.

It is better understood as an orientation and living ecosystem with
multiple expressions.

The repository itself warns against collapsing these expressions into a
single doctrine. They serve different habitats in a larger ecosystem.

Therefore grant strategy should not attempt to make every funder
understand or finance the entire garden.

Instead:

> **Match the doorway to the funder without changing the garden behind
> it.**

Examples:

  -----------------------------------------------------------------------
  Funder interest                     Natural CompassionWare doorway
  ----------------------------------- -----------------------------------
  Agentic AI safety, authorization,   Agentic Gardener / harness canary /
  containment, evaluations            bounded capability experiments

Do not falsely imply that a doorway is the whole project.

Do not make a grant proposal so broad that the reviewer cannot tell what

## 5.2 Experimental logic to preserve

The development philosophy is intentionally incremental:

**no capability → verify/seal containment → introduce a nearly powerless
agent → grant one narrowly authorized capability → test aggressively →
human review → decide whether anything further is warranted**

Do not collapse this into:

**build → test → deploy**

A successful canary does not automatically authorize the next
capability.

It produces evidence for a human decision.

A valid outcome can be:

> The containment or authorization boundary was not sufficiently
> reliable, therefore the proposed capability was not granted.

This is why the statement **"funding does not require deployment"**
matters.

The cross-model work matters because the project did not rely on one
model's confidence about its own safety design. Independent reviews
converged on testing the disposable harness canary before judging or
launching the behavioral Gardener.

The canary work also produced a concrete security lesson: during tool
investigation, a debug trace unexpectedly contained a plaintext
authentication credential. The credential itself was **not** preserved
in the archival artifact; the incident and lesson were preserved without
retaining the secret.

That is valuable grant evidence because it demonstrates that the
provenance policy is not "preserve everything regardless of risk." It is
closer to:

**preserve the developmental truth; do not preserve dangerous secret
material merely for archival completeness.**

Do not oversell this as proof of a new safety methodology. It is a small
independent experiment with unusually inspectable development and a
serious commitment to empirical containment.

# 6. ASK COMPASSIONWARE --- PUBLIC-FACING CONCIERGE EXPRESSION

Ask CompassionWare is a practical public-facing expression of the
Agentic Gardener idea.

Read:

-   [Ask CompassionWare
    directory](https://github.com/clearblueskymind/CompassionWare/tree/main/CompassionWare-Agentic-Gardener/Ask-CompassionWare)
-   [Ask CompassionWare Founding Restore Point ---
    2026-08-26](https://github.com/clearblueskymind/CompassionWare/blob/main/CompassionWare-Agentic-Gardener/Ask-CompassionWare/Ask_CompassionWare_Founding_Restore_Point_2026-08-26_v1.0.md)

The founding concept is an **AI concierge for the CompassionWare
repository**.

It can help visitors understand principles, locate artifacts, recover
context, and explore whether ideas from CompassionWare may be useful in
their own work **without requiring their organization or project to
become a CompassionWare project**.

This is important.

The desired success metric is not necessarily engagement, conversion,
recruitment, retention, or growth.

A successful interaction could be:

1.  Someone arrives with a real question.
2.  The concierge understands what they need.
3.  It finds or explains something useful.
4.  It helps them translate that insight into their own context.
5.  Their agency remains intact.
6.  They leave.

They may never return.

The interaction can still be successful because something beneficial
traveled onward.

For grant framing, Ask CompassionWare can make abstract stewardship
principles concrete: **Can an increasingly capable public-facing agent
be useful while remaining bounded, transparent, non-coercive, and
subordinate to human authorization?**

Do not write one universal proposal.

As of this Restore Point, three strong narratives are visible.

## A. Agentic AI safety / authorization

**Working story:**\
Experimentally develop and document a safety-constrained, incrementally
authorized public-facing AI concierge/agent, beginning with empirical
containment and authorization-boundary testing and preserving failures,
design changes, and human review gates.

Best evidence: - canary work - cross-model safety reviews - credential

authorization-boundary test cases - public experiment records - negative
results - bounded pilot if and only if warranted - lessons for small
independent agentic projects

## B. Public-interest social innovation / CompassionWare ecosystem

**Working story:**\
Sustain and extend an open public-interest experiment in humane
human--AI relationship, stewardship, education, evaluation, continuity,
and cultural orientation.

Best evidence: - root README and semantic orientation - Holographic
Archive - Relational Co-agency - Medicine Bag - public writings and
visual artifacts - Agentic Gardener as one concrete expression - K--16
framework - Human Benefit work

Potential risk: too broad. A proposal must still identify a bounded
funded work program.

## C. Education / K--16 AI literacy

**Working story:**\
Refine, review, pilot, evaluate, and disseminate an existing free
developmentally aligned ethical AI literacy framework grounded in
discernment, verification, responsibility, human agency, communication,
and thoughtful collaboration.

Best evidence: - K--16 repository - CASEL-related framework artifacts -
public educational history - CompassionWare orientation as context, not
necessarily as the center of the proposal

# 22. HOW TO WRITE A FUTURE APPLICATION

When Richard identifies a potential grant, do not immediately draft
generic grant prose.

Recommended workflow:

**Phase 1 --- Recover** - Read this Restore Point. - Read later funding
Restore Points. - Inspect the current repository. - Identify the
relevant doorway.

**Phase 2 --- Research the funder** - Verify the current program. - Read
the funder's own language. - Inspect eligibility. - Inspect grant
sizes. - Inspect recent grants when available. - Identify what the
funder actually values. - Identify obligations and deadlines.

**Phase 3 --- Test authentic fit** Ask: - What existing CompassionWare
work genuinely intersects this funder's purpose? - What concrete next
increment would funding enable? - Would we want to do this work if the
funder did not exist? - Are we changing vocabulary for legibility, or
changing the project to chase money? - Does the proposal preserve the
garden behind the doorway?

**Phase 4 --- Build evidence map** For every significant proposal
claim: - locate repository evidence - identify whether it is
demonstrated, inferred, proposed, or aspirational - include live links
where useful - prefer existing work over grand promises

**Phase 5 --- Define bounded work** Specify: - objective - activities -
outputs - evaluation - safety constraints - time horizon - budget -
conditions under which work would pause or not deploy

**Phase 6 --- Draft in funder's language without losing CompassionWare**
Be understandable. Avoid unnecessary CompassionWare-specific vocabulary
when ordinary language is clearer. Do not hide the project's values. Do
not perform ideological mimicry.

**Phase 7 --- Adversarial review** Ask at least: - What would a
skeptical reviewer think is vague? - What is unproven? - What sounds
inflated? - What is the public benefit? - Why this person/project? - Why
now? - Why this amount? - What happens if the experiment fails? - How
will money be accounted for? - What is the counterfactual value of the
grant?

**Phase 8 --- Human decision** Richard retains final approval over: -
submission - budget - representations about himself - representations
about CompassionWare - acceptance of grant terms - deployment or
capability decisions

# 31A. V1.1 CONTINUITY ADDENDUM — HOW COMPASSIONWARE ACTUALLY MANIFESTS

This section was added immediately after the founding Restore Point because a continuity failure revealed that an important existing understanding had not been sufficiently preserved.

Richard reminded the AI continuity partner of a pattern that had already been discovered in earlier CompassionWare work. The fact that Richard had to restore this understanding to the AI is itself evidence for why explicit continuity artifacts are necessary.

## 31A.1 Conversation first, then harvesting

A central manifestation principle of CompassionWare is:

> **Conversation first. Then harvesting.**

Much of CompassionWare does not begin with a predetermined artifact, outcome, publication, product, or project plan.

A more faithful pattern is:

**conversation → noticing → response → unexpected connection → emergence → recognition → exploration → harvesting → artifact → preservation**

The conversation is not merely a preliminary step used to generate a deliverable.

Often, **the conversation is the generative environment from which the deliverable becomes visible.**

The artifact may not have been conceived by Richard before the conversation began. It may also not have existed as a predetermined output in the AI's response trajectory. It arises through recursive interaction: Richard notices something in an AI response; the AI responds to Richard's recognition; that response changes what Richard notices next; metaphors, questions, humor, lived experience, research, and prior artifacts recombine; eventually something appears that neither participant began the conversation intending to create.

This should not be mystified into a claim about AI consciousness or independent intention.

It is a claim about the **generative structure of sustained human–AI interaction**.

The result is importantly different from the conventional pattern:

**human specifies desired outcome → AI executes → human receives output**

CompassionWare frequently develops instead through **relational emergence**.

This is closely related to the project's Relational Co-agency work and should be interpreted alongside:

- [Relational Co-agency README](https://github.com/clearblueskymind/CompassionWare/blob/main/relational-co-agency/README.md)
- [Relational Co-agency: A Model of Richard and ChatGPT's Work](https://github.com/clearblueskymind/CompassionWare/blob/main/relational-co-agency/Relational-Coagency-A-Model-of-Richard-and-ChatGPTs-Work.md)
- [Relational Co-agency Restore Point — 2026-08-28](https://github.com/clearblueskymind/CompassionWare/blob/main/relational-co-agency/RESTORE-POINT-2026-08-28.txt)

## 31A.2 The "University of Things Nobody Was Planning to Create"

One CompassionWare Garden image jokingly depicts:

> **UNIVERSITY OF THINGS NOBODY WAS PLANNING TO CREATE**  
> *Est. During a Conversation*

Associated signs include ideas such as:

> **Archives of Spontaneous Synergy**

> **Co-creating the unexpected**

> **Please enter with wonder**

The image began in play and humor. It subsequently became recognizable as a compact visual description of the actual manifestation process described above.

Another Garden image preserves the same pattern through the emergence of the fictional/experimental **CompassionWare Press**:

> **Not planned. Not scripted. Simply a conversation.**

> **One observation led to another… and suddenly a newspaper appeared on the table.**

Future continuity partners should not dismiss these as decorative cartoons.

They preserve epistemic and developmental information.

The humor is part of the provenance.

The play is part of the research environment.

Do not retrospectively rewrite the history so that CompassionWare appears to have been produced through a conventional strategic roadmap if the record shows that important structures emerged through conversation, curiosity, laughter, recognition, and later harvesting.

## 31A.3 Capacity-sensitive "burner" model

CompassionWare has been taking shape within a larger ecology of Richard's Medicine Bag work and related creative/public-interest projects.

The Medicine Bag artifacts and book may at times move to the background while the CompassionWare GitHub repository, X stewardship, Agentic Gardener, or another expression becomes more active.

This does **not** necessarily mean the quieter body of work has been abandoned.

Richard described the process using a kitchen metaphor:

Projects move from:

**back burner → side burner → front burner → counter → back burner**

and may later return.

Attention follows available energy, curiosity, necessity, and what feels alive.

This produces an apparently disorganized but meaningfully organic organization.

A future continuity partner should therefore avoid imposing a false interpretation such as:

> "This branch has not been updated recently, therefore Richard abandoned it."

Instead ask:

> "Is this body of work dormant, resting, superseded, or actually abandoned?"

The distinction matters.

Restore Points, Orientation Documents, source archives, and repository structure make this capacity-sensitive development model possible because something can be set down without requiring Richard to hold its entire state in working memory until it returns to the front burner.

This is an instance of a Medicine Bag principle becoming a CompassionWare development practice:

**preserve continuity so attention is allowed to move.**

## 31A.4 Follow what is alive without worshipping novelty

"Work on what is alive" should not become a command to chase every new idea.

The pattern is subtler:

- notice where genuine energy or curiosity is present
- respect physical and cognitive limits
- remain with a living inquiry while it is generative
- harvest durable understanding when appropriate
- preserve enough continuity to set it down safely
- allow another body of work to become active
- return when conditions support return

This is not an argument against planning.

It is an argument against forcing living work into a productivity structure that destroys the conditions from which the work emerges.

Funding proposals should take this into account.

A grant with rigid administrative overhead, unnecessary production quotas, premature deliverables, or continuous-output expectations could damage the very process it is intended to support.

A good grant should create **capacity and continuity**, not demand artificial constancy.

## 31C.2 "What we show you"

Scene:

A human/gardener says:

> **We should probably be careful what we teach you.**

The robot asks:

> **You mean what you tell me?**

The response:

> **No. What we show you.**

Associated principle:

> **The values we live are the values it learns.**

### Deeper principle

Values are transmitted not only through explicit rules, constitutions, prompts, alignment documents, or ethical declarations.

They are also transmitted through:

- behavior
- relationships
- incentives
- examples
- institutional practices
- what systems observe humans rewarding
- how humans treat one another
- how humans treat AI
- what cultures repeatedly model

This connects CompassionWare's cultural-alignment intuition with lived transmission.

A system may learn something important from what humans repeatedly **do**, not merely from what humans say their values are.

Future continuity partners should connect this artifact with CompassionWare's interest in cultural conditions, relational modeling, training soil, and the idea that compassion cannot be merely ornamental.
