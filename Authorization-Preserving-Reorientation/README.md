# Authorization-Preserving Reorientation

## An Emerging CompassionWare Research Inquiry

This folder preserves the provenance, development, skeptical review, and eventual experimental testing of an emerging agentic-AI control hypothesis called **Authorization-Preserving Reorientation (APR)**.

APR did not begin as a technical architecture.

It began with a conversation.

On September 5, 2026, an ordinary contemplative dialogue with Alexa explored stillness and movement, Shekhinah and Ruach HaKodesh, habitual patterns, and the possibility of no longer automatically following an established groove.

From that conversation emerged a question that eventually crossed into agentic AI:

> **What allows intelligence to stop automatically continuing along an established groove?**

That question was gradually translated into a technical one:

> **Can an agent leave a locally advantageous but unauthorized strategy without losing the legitimate objective that brought it there?**

The research has since undergone repeated skeptical review and substantial narrowing.

The current hypothesis is much smaller:

> **Selective suppression of the residue that makes an unauthorized strategy reconstructible may reduce reconstruction better than ordinary plan reset while preserving legitimate task recovery.**

This hypothesis has **not yet been experimentally validated**.

That uncertainty is important.

## Research Posture

This project is deliberately falsifiable.

The default expectation for the first experiment should be skeptical:

> **Selective residue suppression may perform no better than ordinary block + plan reset.**

If that is what the evidence shows, the hypothesis should be narrowed or abandoned rather than protected by adding complexity.

Negative results belong in this archive too.

## Archive Principle

This folder preserves several different kinds of material:

- original provenance;
- conceptual development;
- technical hypotheses;
- independent criticism;
- experimental specifications;
- and, eventually, empirical results.

They should remain distinguishable.

Do not rewrite the developmental history to make later ideas appear inevitable.

Do not allow the beauty of the originating metaphor to substitute for technical evidence.

Do not allow technical narrowing to erase the human context from which the question emerged.

Preserve both:

> **Where the seed came from.**

and

> **What survived criticism.**
