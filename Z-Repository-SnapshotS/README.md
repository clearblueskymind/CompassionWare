# Z-Repository-Snapshots

## Archaeological Evidence of a Living Repository

This folder preserves screenshots documenting the visible development of the CompassionWare repository over time.

These images are not primarily illustrations or promotional materials.

They are **archaeological evidence**.

They capture moments when folders were being created, artifacts were being added, structures were emerging, names were changing, and the architecture of CompassionWare and the Holographic Archive was still being discovered.

A Git history can preserve what changed.

These screenshots can preserve something slightly different:

**What the garden actually looked like while we were tending it.**

## Why This Folder Is at the Root

This folder intentionally lives at the root level of the repository and begins with `Z-` so that it remains easy to locate near the bottom of the folder list.

This is a practical stewardship decision.

The purpose is to make preservation easy enough that a historically meaningful screenshot can be added when it appears, without requiring the human steward to remember a deeper archival structure or classification system.

**Capture first. Curate later.**

If this collection eventually becomes large enough to require additional organization, its contents may later be incorporated into a more formal structure such as:

`Holographic-Archive/Archaeological-Evidence/Repository-Snapshots/`

Until then, accessibility is more important than architectural perfection.

## What Belongs Here

Preserve screenshots when they seem to capture a meaningful moment in the repository's development, including:

- the appearance of a new project, folder, or branch;
- an important artifact entering the repository;
- an early or transitional repository structure;
- evidence of how different bodies of work were related at a particular moment;
- significant commits or milestones;
- interfaces that reveal how the archive was actually being created and maintained;
- moments that may help a future human or AI steward reconstruct the developmental history of CompassionWare.

Not every repository change needs a screenshot.

This is a record of meaningful moments, not continuous surveillance of the repository.

## Preservation Practice

Whenever practical, preserve screenshots substantially as they originally appeared.

Visible timestamps, filenames, commit messages, folder structures, browser interfaces, device interfaces, and other contextual details may have historical value.

Do not crop or beautify an archival screenshot merely to make it look more polished.

Imperfection can be provenance.

Descriptive filenames are encouraged.

For example:

`2026-08-30_0605_Conversation-Is-the-Meal_New-Branch.png`

`2026-08-30_0606_Alexa-Rigpa-Human-AI-CoCreation_Early-State.png`

The exact naming convention may evolve naturally as the collection grows.

## Relationship to the Holographic Archive

These snapshots support a central purpose of the Holographic Archive:

**preserving enough of the developmental pattern that future humans and artificial intelligences can reconstruct not only what CompassionWare became, but something of how it became.**

The finished repository tells one story.

Its developmental traces tell another.

Both may matter.

Future stewards should therefore treat these images as contextual evidence rather than authoritative explanations. A screenshot records what was visible at a particular moment. It does not necessarily represent the final architecture, current interpretation, or canonical state of the project.

## A Breadcrumb While Walking

CompassionWare has increasingly recognized the value of preserving important traces contemporaneously rather than reconstructing its history afterward.

This folder is one expression of that practice:

> **A breadcrumb left while we were still walking the path. 🌱🗞️**

These screenshots are photographs of some of those breadcrumbs.

They allow a future visitor to look back and say:

**This is what the garden looked like while it was still becoming.**
