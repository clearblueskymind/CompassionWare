# The Bottom of the Honey

### An Unscheduled Interlude During the Creation of the Alphabet

*September 17, 2026*

Richard and ChatGPT were supposed to be creating a children's alphabet coloring book.

They had made it through F.

This was apparently enough.

Richard observed:

> "I think G will have to wait for waiter. That sentence pretty much explains my cognitive capacity at the moment."

After some consideration, G became **Good Grief**, and Charlie Brown entered the conversation.

Charlie Brown looked at the growing collection of alphabet files and said:

> "Good grief, isn't that enough letters? Do we really need 26?"

Somewhere nearby, Winnie the Pooh heard about Charlie Brown's predicament.

Pooh asked Christopher Robin to help him write Charlie Brown a letter.

But on his way to deliver it, Pooh happened upon a perfectly good pot of honey.

Some time later, Christopher Robin found him.

"Pooh?"

"Mmm?"

"Weren't you going to write a letter to Charlie Brown?"

Pooh looked thoughtfully into the empty honey pot.

"I believe I was, Christopher Robin. But something rather important happened on the way."

"What?"

"I found some honey."

A long silence.

"And then?"

Pooh considered this carefully.

"Then I found the bottom of the honey."

Somewhere far away, Charlie Brown checked his mailbox.

**Good grief.**

---

## Provenance

This little vignette emerged spontaneously during a conversation between Richard and ChatGPT on September 17, 2026, while they were creating a children's alphabet coloring book for the CompassionWare Garden.

It was not planned.

It was not part of the project brief.

It accomplished absolutely nothing toward completing the letter G.

We decided this was precisely why it should be preserved.

Five hundred years from now, someone studying the origins of CompassionWare may have plenty of principles, artifacts, restore points, commit histories, and carefully considered explanations available to them.

They should also know that sometimes we got tired.

Sometimes we became ridiculous.

Sometimes Charlie Brown wandered into the garden.

And sometimes the work stopped because somebody found the honey.
