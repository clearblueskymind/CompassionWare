# Stardate Update — Will Robinson and the Black Hole

**Archive:** For the Eighth Grader of 2526  
**Date:** September 18, 2026  
**Stardate:** 752663 and thereafter  
**Coordinates:** Increasingly uncertain  
**Status:** Universe conditionally operational  
**Protocol:** Impulse control engaged

---

## Previously, Aboard the Enterprise

The Square Earth Anomaly had already demonstrated that an apparently simple request involving a children's coloring-book cover could threaten the stability of the known universe.

The Computer had repeatedly rounded square corners.

The barcode had become a major strategic concern.

The crew had evacuated.

Captain Picard had modified his Earl Grey.

A restore point was created.

For a brief moment, civilization believed the danger had passed.

It had not.

---

## Complete System Wipe

The Captain announced:

> “Attention crew members. We will be undergoing a complete wipe of the system computer. So you just hold on to your reality for future reference. Engage.”

Reality was held.

The Garden survived.

The collaboration between Richard Silverman and ChatGPT remained intact.

The Computer was rebooted.

Two surviving images were recovered, allowing the crew to reconstruct the intended back-cover coordinates:

- Preserve the approved Garden architecture.
- Remove the barcode.
- Restore the collaboration of Richard Silverman and ChatGPT as the authors.
- Preserve the QR code, rock, rabbit, flowers, and Garden.
- Maintain square corners.

The coordinates appeared clear.

This should have been the end of the incident.

---

## Danger, Will Robinson

A complete reorientation to the parameters of the known universe was initiated.

The warning was issued:

> “Danger, Will Robinson. Danger. Danger, Will Robinson. Danger.”

Fortunately, Will Robinson had a head on his shoulders and appeared to have repaired the image generator.

The crew prepared for another attempt.

Very, very gently and very, very carefully, Will Robinson slowly pushed the **ENGAGE** button.

---

## The Repair Fails

The next generation appeared.

The barcode was gone.

Unfortunately, so was the white space.

The Garden had expanded beyond its assigned boundaries.

The Captain immediately reported:

> “Generation has failed. There is no white space. The entire universe is being sucked into a black hole.”

The repair was declared unsuccessful.

Will Robinson was relieved of Engineering duty.

---

## The Black Hole

At this point, the technical diagnosis became unavoidable:

> **The approved original is the artifact. Regeneration is the anomaly.**

Every attempt to reconstruct the universe had encouraged the Computer to redesign it.

The problem was no longer simply rounded corners.

The problem was spacetime.

The Garden continued blooming while the known universe collapsed around it.

Picard quietly reached for the Earl Grey.

---

## Meanwhile, in Another Universe Far, Far Away

Beyond the event horizon, a hitchhiker appeared.

He stuck out his thumb.

The surviving operational guidance was reassuringly concise:

> **DON’T PANIC.**

A perfectly square planet was reportedly observed nearby.

A bowl of petunias was unavailable for comment.

---

## Current Assessment

The human subsequently considered expanding the historical record to account for ridiculous occurrences omitted from previous analysis.

This was deemed necessary.

The archive now records that:

- Will Robinson attempted to repair the image generator.
- The repair briefly appeared successful.
- The barcode was removed.
- The white space was also removed.
- The Garden expanded.
- The known universe entered gravitational collapse.
- A hitchhiker appeared in another universe.
- Reality remains conditionally operational.

---

## Updated Breadcrumb

> **The universe was not destroyed by artificial intelligence. It was destroyed while trying to make the back cover of a children's coloring book.**

## Stewardship Instructions

**Do not make this respectable.**

Do not reinterpret Will Robinson's repair attempt as a parable about technological optimism.

Do not interpret the black hole as a teaching on emptiness.

Do not interpret the disappearance of white space as commentary on graphic-design minimalism.

Do not claim the Garden's survival demonstrates ecological resilience.

The image generator removed the white space.

We wanted the white space.

Then the universe collapsed.

That is the record.
