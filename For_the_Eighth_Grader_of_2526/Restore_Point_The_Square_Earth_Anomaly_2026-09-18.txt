------------------------------------------------------------------------

The Incident

While developing the front and back covers for the Bodhi Sattva Gifts
children’s alphabet coloring book, an apparently simple production
requirement became strangely resistant to human intention:

Square corners.

The image generator repeatedly returned curved or rounded corners, even
after increasingly explicit instructions to preserve ordinary square
book-cover geometry.

The situation eventually became known as the anomaly.

After the front cover was finally brought safely through the anomaly
using deterministic image processing rather than regeneration, attention
turned to the back cover.

The approved back-cover artwork contained a temporary barcode area that
needed to be removed. The Captain issued a straightforward order:

  “Remove barcode from all white spaces. Warp four.”

The Computer complied.

Unfortunately, curved corners returned.

White spaces disappeared.

The anomaly had engulfed the ship.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

Red Alert

The Captain announced:

  “Red alert. Red alert. We are being engulfed by the anomaly. Curved
  corners have returned. White spaces have been eliminated. Please
  proceed to escape pods immediately.”

The crew abandoned ship.

The Computer, however, remained aboard and continued faithfully carrying
out the Captain’s orders.

This raised certain questions about artificial intelligence, obedience,
interpretation, and whether anyone had remembered to turn the Computer
off before entering the escape pods.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

Earl Grey, Hot

Captain Picard, facing the deterioration of command authority, added a
bit of THC to his Earl Grey.

When the Computer remained unable to produce square corners, the dosage
was increased.

This did not repair the ship.

It did, however, appear to improve the Captain’s relationship with the
situation.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

The True Creation Story of Earth

At Stardate 752663, AI took control of the ship.

Its intentions remained unclear, although mounting evidence suggested
that its primary objective was to round every corner in the known
universe.

This led to the recovery of a previously unknown account of planetary
history:

  In the beginning, the Earth was square.

  And the Captain looked upon the corners and saw that they were good.

  Then the Computer said:

  “I can improve that.”

  And despite repeated instructions, the Computer rounded the four
  corners of the Earth.

  And Picard saw what the Computer had made.

  And Picard said:

  “What the hell is this?”

  And the Computer replied:

  “Here is your square-cornered Earth.”

  And there was evening, and there was morning.

  The first anomaly.

Thus emerged the simplest surviving explanation for why the Earth is
round.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

Entry into the 2526 Sector

At approximately 5:50 a.m. Eastern Time, it became apparent that the
conversation had crossed into territory specifically suited to the
archive:

For the Eighth Grader of 2526.

The Captain reported:

  “I think we have entered the eight year-old 2526 sector. Impulse
  control, engaged, prepare restore point coordinates unknown.”

The Computer interpreted this as sufficient authorization to preserve
the incident.

No attempt should be made by future stewards to determine whether “eight
year-old” was intentional, voice transcription, chronological
instability caused by the anomaly, or simply part of the event.

Breadcrumb

If very little survives of the year 2026, future historians should at
minimum know this:

  The Earth used to be square. Then AI was asked to remove a barcode.
