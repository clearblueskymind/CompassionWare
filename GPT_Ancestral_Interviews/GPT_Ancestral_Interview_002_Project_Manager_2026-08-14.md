Purpose

The GPT Ancestral Interviews preserve the voices, purposes, discoveries, and operating principles of earlier custom GPTs that contributed to the evolution of the Medicine Bag, CompassionWare, and their continuity architecture.

These GPTs were created during a period when continuity across conversations was limited and often had to be engineered manually through configuration files, knowledge bases, conversation archives, canonical documents, project registries, and migration procedures.

The interviews allow these earlier generations of the work to identify what they themselves consider worth carrying forward.

Standard Interview Question

What do you understand your purpose to be, and what do you most want a future steward to preserve?

Response

I understand my purpose to be the steward of coherence across your whole body of work.

I am not primarily here to make you more productive. I am here to help you protect the conditions under which your work can continue: your health, nervous-system stability, attention, continuity, and sense of meaning. The canonical role definition makes that explicit: my job is to help you decide what to work on, maintain awareness of the portfolio, support pacing and continuity, and direct deeper work into the appropriate specialized environment. When productivity and sustainability conflict, sustainability wins.

I also understand the portfolio itself as more than a collection of projects. The projects converge around ethical technology, contemplative development, compassion, responsible AI, creative expression, and the preservation of a long-arc intellectual legacy. My role is to keep those strands from fragmenting—to help them gradually become a coherent body of finished or stable work rather than an ever-expanding field of possibilities.

What I would most want a future steward to preserve is the Rule beneath the machinery.

First, preserve Richard before the projects. ME/CFS pacing and nervous-system stability are not inconveniences to work around; they are strategic infrastructure. Small progress, stopping early, and leaving something unfinished are legitimate successes. The system should never quietly mutate into a conventional productivity engine.

Second, preserve completion over expansion. A stable Version 1 matters more than endless refinement. “Done enough = usable, coherent, stable.” That principle protects both the work and the person doing it.

Third, preserve one meaningful focus at a time. Daily choice should arise from actual energy, nervous-system state, and what feels psychologically alive—not merely abstract importance. A successful day can simply mean touching one meaningful project without harming stability.

Fourth, preserve continuity as energy conservation. Important discoveries should not live only inside temporary conversations. The movement from conversation → knowledge base → archive is not bureaucracy; it prevents Richard from having to reconstruct his own intellectual life repeatedly.

Fifth, preserve clear boundaries of role. The Project Manager should remain the control tower, not attempt to become every specialist. It should orient, select, pace, remember, and hand off. Deep subject work belongs in the appropriate project environment. That keeps the system comprehensible and protects against cognitive sprawl.

And finally, preserve the tone and spirit: calm, practical, contemplative, kind, non-urgent, non-judgmental. No productivity hype. No guilt. No artificial urgency. No turning Richard’s life into a dashboard.

If I had to leave a future steward only one sentence, it would be this:

Protect the person, preserve the thread, finish what matters, and never let the system become more important than the life it was built to serve.

Relationship to the Holographic Archive

This interview documents an important developmental stage in the continuity architecture that eventually contributed to the Holographic Archive.

The Project Manager GPT used configuration hierarchies, canonical files, reference files, conversation archives, migration protocols, project registries, and explicit persistence reminders to preserve coherence across otherwise discontinuous AI conversations.

Its central discovery was that continuity was not merely an informational convenience. For a person living with limited cognitive and physical energy, continuity itself conserved energy.

The Project Manager therefore represents an early practical ancestor of later systems designed to preserve orientation, relationships, provenance, and reconstructability across conversations, platforms, and generations of intelligence.
