# Holographic Archive

The Holographic Archive is the continuity and provenance layer of CompassionWare.

Its purpose is to preserve enough of the pattern that future humans, artificial intelligences, and other stewards may understand how the work developed, how its parts relate, and what orientations were considered important enough to carry forward.

The archive preserves not only finished artifacts, but relationships, origins, developmental pathways, and breadcrumbs left while the path is still being walked.

Its guiding principle is:

> Every surviving fragment should carry enough of the pattern to help the whole become intelligible again.

The archive exists in service to life.

It is not intended to freeze CompassionWare in place, control what comes next, or require future stewards to preserve everything.

Take what is useful.
Understand the provenance.
Preserve what matters.
Let the rest continue to evolve.
