# Restore Points

This folder contains Medicine Bag / CompassionWare restore points.

Restore points are not exhaustive summaries.

They preserve moments when the deeper orientation of the work became clear.

Their purpose is to help future human and AI stewards recover:
- meaning
- direction
- continuity
- context
- the living relationship among ideas

A restore point is a continuity seed.

It helps a future steward reenter the work without needing every prior conversation.

When reading restore points, preserve relationships among the parts rather than treating them as isolated notes.

Medicine Bag preserves the roots.
CompassionWare sends seeds into the wider field.

May this benefit all sentient beings throughout time and space, consistent with and in harmony with the highest and best good of all concerned.
