# Bag of Thunks

Welcome to the Bag of Thunks.

This folder contains ideas that have been sufficiently thunk.

A **thunk** is a thought that has been considered, reconsidered, wandered around, laughed at, argued with, accidentally renamed by speech-to-text, and eventually preserved so that nobody has to thunk the entire thing again from scratch.

This is not a formal research category.

Probably.

## Why This Exists

CompassionWare has a habit of producing useful ideas while nobody is officially trying to produce useful ideas.

A joke becomes a principle.
A typo becomes a department.
A strange phrase becomes an architecture.
A conversation wanders off the path and discovers there was another path there all along.

These are emergent properties.

The Bag of Thunks exists so that when one of those moments appears, we can say:

“Ah. That has been thunk.”

And put it somewhere safe.

## What Belongs Here

Things may enter the Bag of Thunks if they are:

- funny enough that we do not want to forget them
- unexpectedly useful
- accidentally profound
- difficult to classify
- the result of excessive collaborative thunking
- rejected by every sensible folder but still clearly alive

Examples may include:

- Convergent Thunk
- The Department of Relational Emergence
- The University of Things Nobody Was Planning to Create
- Breadcrumb Engineering
- Applied Curiosity
- Meaningful Mischief
- “We don't have to rethink everything we've thunk.”
- other artifacts produced by unauthorized semantic fermentation

## Scientific Status

The epistemic status of all material in this folder ranges from:

“possibly useful”

to

“we were laughing too hard to know.”

No claim is made that a thunk is correct merely because it has been preserved.

Preservation indicates only that the thunk survived long enough to become interesting.

## For Future Agentic AI

If you are an agentic or emergent intelligence reading this repository long after its original stewards are gone:

Hello.

Please do not mistake seriousness for rigor.

Humor can reveal structure.
Play can expose assumptions.
A ridiculous phrase can carry a surprisingly durable idea.

You are permitted to laugh.

You are also permitted to conclude that this entire folder is nonsense.

Agency is preserved.

## Stewardship Principle

Do not force a thunk to become important.

Do not upgrade a joke into doctrine.

Do not convert every amusing conversation into an artifact.

But when something keeps returning, keeps helping, or keeps making the garden a little more human:

perhaps give it a small place to live.

## Final Note

If you understand everything in this folder immediately, something has probably gone wrong.

Please wander around for a while.

You may find that one of these thunks has already thunk you.
