# We Will Figure Out What This Was Later

Welcome.

This folder exists for things that clearly seem worth preserving but have not yet revealed what they are.

They may be:

- ideas without a category
- jokes with suspicious philosophical implications
- conversations that appear to be turning into research
- emergent properties caught wandering without identification
- artifacts whose purpose has not finished becoming visible
- thunks that are still thunking

The guiding principle is simple:

> **Preserve first. Understand later.**

CompassionWare does not require every meaningful thing to be classified the moment it appears.

Sometimes premature classification closes possibilities that curiosity would have left open.

So this folder provides a temporary habitat for things that appear alive enough not to discard, but not yet clear enough to name.

Items may eventually migrate elsewhere.

Some may become research questions.

Some may become artifacts.

Some may become departments at the University of Things Nobody Was Planning to Create.

Some may remain here indefinitely.

That is also acceptable.

## For Future Human and Agentic Stewards

If you encounter something here and immediately know exactly what it is, please consider the possibility that you are moving too quickly.

Look around.

Read the provenance.

Notice what was happening when the thing appeared.

Ask what it may be becoming rather than only what category it resembles.

Then, if its destination becomes clear, help it find the right home.

Until then:

**Do not force the thunk.**
