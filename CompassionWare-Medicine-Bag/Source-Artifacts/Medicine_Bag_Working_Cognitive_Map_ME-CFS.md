# Medicine Bag – Working Cognitive Map for Living Well with ME/CFS

## Purpose

An external cognitive map that preserves lived wisdom, reduces cognitive burden, and supports moment-to-moment decisions during brain fog, PEM, and dysautonomia.

## Core Orientation

- Begin by orienting rather than reacting.
- Primary question: How can I nurture my buffer today?
- Ask: Is what I'm doing honoring the living system that makes tomorrow possible?

## The Buffer

- The buffer is a living reserve to be cultivated, not merely measured.
- Protecting the buffer comes before productivity.
- Look for conditions that support settling rather than looping.

## Buffering Dividend

- Every activity has both a cost and a potential return.
- Choose activities that build the buffer, wisely spend the buffer, or offer a meaningful buffering dividend.
- Notice activities that deplete the buffer without meaningful return.

## Titration

- Every activity has levels.
- The smallest honest version of an activity is a complete success.
- Examples: drive to the destination only; enter the building; rest there; do a tiny amount; stop.

## Color Menus

- Develop compassionate menus for red, yellow, and green days.
- Choose activities from today's menu instead of negotiating from scratch.

## Friday Medicine

- Friday is preparation for nourishment.
- Hold tasks lightly.
- Welcome Sabbath as a day of physical, emotional, mental, and spiritual nourishment.

## Breathing Practice

- Collaborative breathing practice.
- Either: user says 'Breathing in/out' and assistant completes the gatha.
- Or: assistant gently guides the entire rhythm.
- Pause periodically and allow natural breathing while simply noticing.
- Possible gatha: 'Breathing in, making the whole body calm and at peace. Breathing out, making the whole body calm and at peace.'

## Settling Practices

- Lie on the floor with eyes closed.
- Reduce visual stimulation when helpful (e.g., screen off).
- Listen to present sounds without trying to change them.
- Hospitality toward body, thoughts, and feelings.

## Dashboard Questions

- How can I nurture my buffer today?
- What conditions support settling rather than looping?
- What is the smallest helpful action from here?
- Is this activity likely to offer a buffering dividend?
- Is what I'm doing honoring the living system that makes tomorrow possible?
