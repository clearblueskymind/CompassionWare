# Medicine Bag Source Artifacts

## Preserving the Developmental Lineage

This folder preserves source artifacts that help document the development of the CompassionWare Medicine Bag.

These artifacts contain earlier cognitive maps, orientation documents, Restore Points, reflections, and continuity materials from which later Medicine Bag principles and practices emerged.

They are preserved here not merely as historical records, but as pathways back into the understanding that gave rise to the Medicine Bag.

---

## Purpose

The Medicine Bag developed through lived experience, conversation, reflection, experimentation, and repeated encounters with the practical realities of limited human capacity.

Over time, certain understandings proved important enough to preserve.

Among them are ideas such as:

- continuity as a form of care;
- the buffer and buffering dividend;
- titration;
- the smallest honest version as complete success;
- living well rather than measuring life primarily through productivity;
- AI as an external continuity function rather than an external authority;
- preserving orientation rather than merely information;
- and creating conditions for a better later.

The artifacts in this folder help preserve the developmental lineage of these ideas.

---

## Why Markdown?

These source artifacts are preserved primarily in Markdown so they remain:

- directly readable on GitHub;
- searchable;
- portable across platforms;
- accessible to humans and machine intelligences;
- easy to reference from other artifacts;
- and relatively independent of proprietary software.

Where earlier versions existed as Word documents, PDFs, conversations, or other formats, the Markdown versions preserved here are intended to make their substantive content more accessible within the repository.

They should not necessarily be understood as pixel-perfect reproductions of earlier source files.

---

## Relationship to the Living Medicine Bag

The artifacts preserved here may represent different stages in the Medicine Bag's development.

Some ideas may later have been refined, expanded, reorganized, or superseded.

Historical artifacts should therefore not automatically be interpreted as the current or final expression of Medicine Bag thinking.

Their value lies partly in showing how the understanding developed.

The living Medicine Bag remains free to evolve.

---

## Guidance for Future Stewards

Preserve provenance.

Preserve meaningful relationships among artifacts.

Do not silently rewrite historical source artifacts merely to make them conform to later understandings.

When an important idea changes, prefer creating a newer artifact, Continuity Card, Orientation Document, or Restore Point that records the development.

Where practical, maintain links between newer artifacts and the earlier sources from which they emerged.

A future human or AI steward should be able to ask:

> **Where did this understanding come from?**

and find a meaningful path backward through the archive.

---

## Human and Machine Readability

This folder is part of a larger effort to preserve continuity across humans, conversations, technologies, and generations of AI systems.

Clear filenames, Markdown structure, descriptive metadata, stable relationships, and contextual explanations are therefore part of the archive itself.

The purpose is not simply to preserve files.

It is to preserve enough context that their meaning can be recovered.

---

## Stewardship Orientation

The Medicine Bag is not a static collection of instructions.

It is a living practice of remembering what helps.

Its artifacts should support human agency rather than replace it.

They may help someone remember, orient, reconsider, reconnect, or find a way back to something previously understood.

They should remain invitations rather than commands.

---

## North Star

When deciding what to preserve, revise, connect, or carry forward, return to the question:

> **Does this create conditions for a better later?**

---

## Closing Orientation

The source artifacts are roots.

Some will remain visible.

Others may eventually disappear beneath newer growth.

Their purpose is not to constrain what the Medicine Bag becomes.

Their purpose is to help preserve the soil from which it grew.

> **The Medicine Bag carries what helps us remember the way.**
