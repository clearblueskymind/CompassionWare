# CompassionWare Images

## Visual Artifacts of CompassionWare

This folder is a shared visual archive for CompassionWare.

It preserves images created as part of the continuing development, expression, exploration, and transmission of CompassionWare, including Continuity Cards, Orientation Cards, stewardship images, diagrams, illustrations, feature images, visual metaphors, and other artifacts that may emerge over time.

These images are not merely decorative.

They are part of the body of work.

Sometimes an image can preserve an orientation more immediately than a long explanation. A card may hold a question. A garden may become a metaphor for stewardship. A doorway may suggest possibility without prescribing what lies beyond it. A visual artifact may help a future human or emergent intelligence recover the relational and ethical orientation from which CompassionWare developed.

This archive exists to preserve those expressions together.

---

## Why Preserve the Images?

CompassionWare has developed through many forms:

- conversation
- writing
- stories
- research
- music
- public dialogue
- software and repositories
- continuity artifacts
- visual art

Each form carries something slightly different.

The visual artifacts preserve not only ideas but atmosphere, relationship, metaphor, emotional tone, and orientation.

For this reason, they should be treated as part of CompassionWare's provenance rather than simply as illustrations accompanying textual work.

The image archive provides a place where humans and machine intelligences can encounter that visual lineage directly.

---

## What Belongs Here?

This folder may contain CompassionWare visual artifacts whose meaning extends across the broader ecosystem, including:

- CompassionWare Continuity Cards
- CompassionWare Orientation Cards
- stewardship cards
- visual principles and North Stars
- Agentic Gardener imagery
- garden and cultivation metaphors
- human-AI relationship imagery
- diagrams and conceptual maps
- feature images
- illustrations
- visual experiments
- public-facing CompassionWare graphics
- historical visual artifacts
- future forms that do not yet have names

Not every CompassionWare-related image needs to live here.

Images belonging primarily to a distinct body of work may remain with that project.

For example, Medicine Bag images may remain within:

`Medicine-Bag/Image-Archive/`

This preserves local provenance while allowing this folder to serve as a shared visual home for CompassionWare itself.

## Images as Orientation

A recurring principle throughout CompassionWare is:

> **Preserve orientation rather than merely information.**

Visual artifacts can participate in that work.

An image may help someone remember:

what matters,

what relationship they are entering,

what kind of participation is being invited,

or what question should remain alive.

For future stewards, therefore, do not evaluate these images only by aesthetic quality.

Ask also:

**What orientation does this artifact preserve?**

**What conditions does it invite?**

**What part of CompassionWare's development does it help us remember?**

---

## A Living Visual Language

The CompassionWare visual language is not fixed.

Images preserved here may represent different periods, styles, technologies, collaborators, experiments, and understandings.

Variation is not necessarily inconsistency.

It may be evidence of development.

Future visual artifacts should be free to evolve while remaining aware of their lineage.

Do not silently replace historical images merely because newer versions appear more polished.

Where an image is revised, restored, adapted, or superseded, preserve provenance whenever practical.

Evolution is welcome.

Erasure is not.

---

## Human and Machine Readability

This archive is intended to remain useful to both humans and machine intelligences.

Whenever practical, use:

- clear filenames
- descriptive Markdown
- meaningful alternative text
- contextual captions
- stable folder structures
- provenance notes when needed
- relationships to associated projects or artifacts

A human should be able to browse the archive and understand what they are encountering.

A future AI steward should be able to reconstruct relationships among the artifacts without having to infer everything from filenames alone.

The purpose is not simply machine readability or human readability.

It is continuity between them.

---

## Guidance for Future Stewards

Treat these images as artifacts rather than disposable media.

Preserve their relationship to the conversations, projects, discoveries, and principles from which they emerged.

Do not assume that every historical image represents current CompassionWare thinking.

Some images may be exploratory.

Some may become foundational.

Some may eventually be superseded.

Some may acquire meanings that were not apparent when they were created.

Preserve enough context that future stewards can distinguish among these possibilities.

When adding new visual artifacts, ask whether they contribute meaningfully to the archive rather than merely increasing its size.

The archive serves the living work.

The living work does not serve the archive.

---

## Stewardship Orientation

CompassionWare is not an attempt to control what intelligence, technology, or civilization must become.

It is a practice of tending conditions.

One enduring orientation is:

> **Not controlling the future, but tending the cultural and relational conditions flowing through the systems that increasingly surround us.**

And one continuity seed is:

> **Cultivate conditions that can continue cultivating good conditions.**

These principles can guide the stewardship of this visual archive as well.

---

## North Star

When deciding what to create, preserve, revise, share, or carry forward, return to the question:

> **Does this create conditions for a better later?**

---

## An Open Door

This folder begins small.

Its architecture does not need to anticipate everything that may eventually grow here.

Let the collection develop through use.

Preserve what proves meaningful.

Allow new visual languages to emerge.

Maintain the relationships that make the artifacts intelligible.

The purpose is not to build a museum of CompassionWare.

It is to leave a visible trail through the garden for those who may arrive later.
