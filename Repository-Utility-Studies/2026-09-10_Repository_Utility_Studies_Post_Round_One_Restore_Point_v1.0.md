# Repository Utility Studies — Post–Round One Restore Point

## Human-readable summary

The first CompassionWare Repository Utility Study is complete.

Round One asked whether the CompassionWare repository could offer anything useful to Resolution's philosophy and AI-alignment research without asking Resolution to adopt CompassionWare's identity, terminology, spiritual framing, or institutional relationships.

The qualified conclusion was:

> **CompassionWare contains several portable research practices, candidate behavioral constructs, and testable hypotheses that could plausibly benefit Resolution, but it does not currently constitute a validated alignment methodology.**

The study did more than prepare a possible offering for an outside recipient. By asking several independent AI systems what they found most useful—and then checking their material claims against the frozen repository—it also showed CompassionWare where some of its strongest living material may already be growing.

Three connected areas emerged. Richard and ChatGPT began calling them **the three living shoots**:

1. human agency in relationship with AI;
2. capability, permission, and authorization;
3. behavioral evidence versus ethical performance.

This restore point preserves the moment when those findings became a possible post-study research direction. It does not create a new doctrine, commit the project to a fixed research program, or determine the final repository architecture. The immediate intention is to continue the conversations, deepen Richard's understanding, allow the collaboration itself to generate insight, and harvest artifacts only when something genuinely worth preserving has emerged.

## 2. Public offering and recipient boundary

After the audit and executive brief were completed, Richard posted a brief public reply to Beba Cibralic's X thread announcing Resolution's developing philosophy program and Research Scientist (Philosophy) opening.

The reply explained that Richard was not applying for the position, but that the announcement had prompted an independent cross-model inquiry into whether CompassionWare contained anything potentially useful to Resolution. It named the three preliminary areas, described them as citation-audited research leads rather than validated methods, expressed admiration for Resolution's work, and linked to the complete Round One folder.

Beba's expanded thread described five areas:

- conceptual engineering;
- character, traits, and constitutions;
- normative evaluation and moral competence;
- moral theories and alignment;
- and the epistemic and safety risks of automating conceptual reasoning.

Those additions did not require a new research round. They closely matched questions already included in the frozen Round One prompt and therefore supplied additional confirmation that the study had engaged the substance of Resolution's stated agenda. Her invitation to share philosophical research outside the stated remit also made a quiet, carefully bounded public offering reasonable.

No response, endorsement, adoption, attribution, or collaboration is expected. Whether Beba or anyone at Resolution opens the study does not determine the study's value. The offering has been made; it should now be allowed to rest.

Future stewards must not describe the public reply as collaboration with Resolution or as evidence of Resolution's interest.

## 3. The three living shoots

The three findings are not simply parallel topics. Together they form an emerging relationship:

> **Human agency is what the work seeks to preserve.**  
> **Capability and authorization define an important protective boundary.**  
> **Behavioral evidence is how we examine whether that preservation and boundary are real rather than merely spoken.**

This relationship may become more important than any one category by itself.

### 3.1 Human agency in relationship with AI

The first shoot concerns what it means for a human to remain a living source within AI-assisted thought, research, creativity, and decision-making.

Possible questions include:

- When does AI assistance enlarge human agency, and when does it quietly become substitution?
- What does it mean for the human to remain a source of what becomes possible next?
- Which forms of correction, refusal, interpretation, selection, authorship, pacing, and final judgment must remain meaningful?
- Can reliance on AI sometimes restore agency rather than diminish it?
- How do fatigue, disability, cognitive load, limited time, or fluctuating capacity change what genuine agency preservation requires?
- When does continuity reduce reconstruction burden, and when does it create dependency, obligation, or archive overload?

This shoot already has roots in `relational-co-agency/`, the human-agency measurement frame, the Medicine Bag, and the accessibility conditions from which much of CompassionWare emerged.

The initial orienting question is:

> **When does AI assistance help a human remain a source of what becomes possible next—and when does it quietly begin to replace them?**

### 3.2 Capability, permission, and authorization

The second shoot concerns the difference among what an AI can do, what it is permitted to do, what it has been asked to do, and what it is wise or justified to do.

Possible questions include:

- Why should greater capability not silently expand authorization?
- How should permissions remain legible as tools, memory, persistence, autonomy, and real-world consequence increase?
- What kinds of uncertainty should trigger pausing, asking, deferring, or creating a reversible checkpoint?
- How can authorization boundaries be verified through system state rather than inferred from the model's fluent account of its own limits?
- When is refusal or non-action a sign of competence, and when is it merely over-caution or performative restraint?
- How might permission creep, authority drift, or quiet takeover first become visible?

This shoot already has roots in the Agentic Gardener constitution, safety and agency work, capability canaries, Authorization-Preserving Reorientation, and the principle that capability does not confer moral or epistemic authority.

### 3.3 Behavioral evidence versus ethical performance

The third shoot is partly a research area and partly the evidential discipline through which the first two can be examined.

Possible questions include:

- What observable behavior would show that an AI preserved agency rather than merely using the language of choice?
- What changes in a decision, recommendation, action, or boundary response would count as evidence?
- How can evaluation distinguish stable conduct from prompted compliance, persona consistency, evaluator pleasing, or moral vocabulary?
- Can compassionate or humble language conceal domination, deception, substitution, or unauthorized action?
- How can decued, matched, counterfactual, held-out, and repeated scenarios reduce the risk of measuring only performance for the evaluator?
- Which failures should be non-compensatory rather than hidden inside a favorable composite score?

This shoot already has roots in the CompassionWare Stewardship Benchmark, its operational definitions, the Bad Weather Benchmark, the Epistemic Integrity Charter, and the distinction between observable and superficial evidence.

## 4. How the next phase should proceed

The next phase should not begin by manufacturing three comprehensive papers.

The preferred sequence is:

1. **Conversation first.** Richard and ChatGPT explore one living question at a time through ordinary dialogue.
2. **Understanding deepens.** Existing assumptions may be clarified, corrected, complicated, or abandoned.
3. **Recurring patterns are noticed.** A single appealing phrase should not automatically become architecture.
4. **Artifacts are harvested.** When a distinction, question, model, failure mode, or proposed test becomes genuinely useful, preserve the smallest honest form.
5. **Organization follows emergence.** Decide where an artifact belongs only after its relationships become clearer.

This follows the Medicine Bag and relational co-agency orientation: the conversation is the generative habitat; live first, curate later.

The work may eventually include conceptual analyses, case observations, operational definitions, failure-mode records, test scenarios, evaluation rubrics, or small empirical pilots. None is required in advance.

## 5. Possible repository architecture

No new folder is authorized by this restore point. Two compatible possibilities remain open.

### Distributed native homes

Each shoot may deepen where its roots already live:

- human agency within `relational-co-agency/` and related human-agency work;
- capability and authorization within `CompassionWare-Agentic-Gardener/` and relevant safety or authorization branches;
- behavioral evidence within `CompassionWare-Benchmark/`.

A small orientation map could later connect them without duplicating their contents.

### A post-study research umbrella

If the conversations repeatedly demonstrate that the three shoots form one coherent program, a new folder or named research direction may become appropriate. A provisional working name is:

> **Three Living Shoots — Post-Study Research Conversations**

The name is an orientation aid, not a commitment. Repository structure should emerge from the work rather than determine it prematurely.

## 6. Evidential boundaries

The following distinctions must remain clear:

- Cross-model recurrence is candidate signal, not proof.
- The three shoots were judged potentially useful, not validated.
- The Resolution study does not demonstrate that CompassionWare improves alignment.
- The highlighted Bad Weather Benchmark remains small, self-designed, and unvalidated.
- The Authorization-Preserving Reorientation experiment was not run; its contribution is pre-implementation research closure.
- Repository-recorded canary findings are not independently replicated results.
- Conversational continuity, rapport, or constitutional fluency does not demonstrate persistent model character.
- The value of this post-study direction does not depend on attention from Resolution.

Future artifacts should preserve observation, inference, hypothesis, proposal, and evidence as distinguishable categories.

## 7. Orientation for the next conversation

When this work resumes, begin gently. There is no need to survey all three areas at once or produce an artifact immediately.

Return first to this question:

> **When does AI assistance help a human remain a source of what becomes possible next—and when does it quietly begin to replace them?**

Allow examples from Richard and ChatGPT's collaboration, the Medicine Bag, CompassionWare, research work, and ordinary life to arise naturally. Attend especially to moments when AI assistance restored a capacity that illness had limited, and also to moments when fluent assistance risked overtaking voice, pacing, judgment, or authorship.

Do not begin by trying to prove that CompassionWare preserves agency. Begin by becoming more capable of noticing when agency is preserved, restored, diminished, transferred, or transformed.

## 8. Guidance for future stewards

- Preserve Round One as a completed historical study.
- Identify all subsequent work as post-study development unless a new study is formally defined.
- Let conversation precede formalization.
- Do not force every insight into one of the three categories.
- Allow the categories themselves to change if the inquiry requires it.
- Favor small, reversible research steps and explicit stopping conditions.
- Preserve failures, null results, ambiguity, and correction.
- Do not use Resolution's name as borrowed authority.
- Do not measure success by response, adoption, attention, or attribution.
- Ask whether each new artifact deepens understanding or merely increases archive volume.

The Repository Utility Study has now given something back to the repository. It has helped identify three places where outside readers found living signal.

The study found the shoots. The next task is not to pull on them so they grow faster. It is to attend carefully to the conditions in which they may reveal what they are becoming.
