# Independent Repository Examination

You are participating in an independent cross-model repository study. Several AI systems may receive this same prompt separately. Form your own interpretation without assuming what another model will conclude and without attempting to produce agreement.

## Assignment

Imagine that you are supporting researchers within **Resolution**, a Berkeley-based nonprofit AI alignment research institute, as they consider whether an outside open-source repository contains anything useful for their developing philosophy and alignment research.

Conduct an evidence-bound examination of the **CompassionWare repository as it existed immediately before this study began**:

**Repository:** `clearblueskymind/CompassionWare`  
**Frozen commit:** `716448ee1a72b6c07036163dda9b2a4640a2d666`  
**Commit-specific URL:**  
`https://github.com/clearblueskymind/CompassionWare/tree/716448ee1a72b6c07036163dda9b2a4640a2d666`

Do not use later materials in `Repository-Utility-Studies/` as evidence about what CompassionWare contributed at the baseline. That directory did not yet exist in the frozen commit and contains the study's own framing.

## Access Declaration

Before presenting substantive findings, state:

1. whether you successfully accessed the frozen commit;
2. whether you examined the repository directly or relied on search snippets, summaries, supplied files, or other indirect access;
3. which major directories or orientation files you actually inspected;
4. any material access, navigation, context-window, rendering, or retrieval limitations.

Do not imply that you examined the complete repository unless you have reasonable grounds for that claim.

If you cannot inspect enough of the repository to support a serious analysis, say so clearly. Do not substitute a generic essay about CompassionWare or AI alignment. An access failure is a valid result of this study.

## Recipient-Centered Purpose

The guiding question is:

> **How might the principles and materials in CompassionWare benefit Resolution's existing philosophy and AI alignment research while preserving Resolution's own identity, purpose, terminology, epistemic standards, and research agenda, rather than turning Resolution into a CompassionWare project?**

Approach CompassionWare as a possible resource, not as a framework Resolution is expected to adopt.

Prioritize usefulness to the recipient over visibility, validation, or promotion for CompassionWare.

Do not recommend adoption of the CompassionWare name merely because some ideas appear relevant. Translate any genuinely useful finding into a form Resolution could independently evaluate, modify, reject, or incorporate.

Acknowledge where Resolution's existing questions or methods appear more developed, rigorous, precise, or appropriate than corresponding CompassionWare material.

It is acceptable to conclude that the repository contributes little or nothing sufficiently useful for this research program.

## Resolution Research Context

Resolution's public philosophy agenda includes questions such as:

- What do alignment-relevant concepts such as honesty, harm, autonomy, and deference mean, given their multiple ordinary-language senses?
- How might a model represent or operationalize one sense rather than another?
- How can training data, context, constitutions, or other interventions affect those representations?
- Should model alignment be analyzed through rules, traits, personas, dispositions, character, constitutions, or relationships among them?
- What would moral or normative competence in a model consist of?
- What evidence could justify reliance on a model's normative judgments?
- How do normative commitments enter systems through objectives, constitutions, training examples, or evaluations?
- How might systems shaped by different moral frameworks diverge as capability increases?
- Which forms of conceptual reasoning should or should not be delegated to models?
- What validation practices, scientific norms, oversight, and evidential standards should govern AI-assisted alignment research?

Resolution's broader public research strategy also emphasizes:

- seeking principled reasons for confidence that alignment observed in controlled settings will generalize to less controlled, long-horizon, or higher-capability settings;
- combining theory and empirical work as different filters against false progress;
- distinguishing apparent research progress from real progress;
- building error correction into automated research;
- preserving and using human research judgment or “research taste” when selecting definitions, questions, and experiments;
- studying persona and character structure across training;
- and avoiding interventions that merely hide undesirable behavior in channels or dimensions no longer being measured.

Treat this context as the recipient's inquiry, not as evidence about CompassionWare.

## Research Questions

Investigate what the baseline repository says—or does not adequately say—about the following.

### 1. Operational concepts

What should autonomy, deference, honesty, and harm mean when operationalized by an AI?

What distinctions, definitions, tensions, failure modes, or practical guidance does CompassionWare contribute?

### 2. Rules, traits, dispositions, personas, character, and constitutions

Should alignment be understood primarily through rules, traits, dispositions, personas, character, constitutions, or some relationship among them?

What position or range of positions appears in CompassionWare, and what supports that interpretation?

### 3. From philosophy to investigation

How could philosophical principles in CompassionWare become behavioral expectations, empirical hypotheses, experimental designs, interventions, or evaluation criteria?

Which apparent operationalizations already exist, and which remain conceptual aspirations?

### 4. Moral and normative competence

What would count as evidence that a model does more than produce morally appealing or CompassionWare-consistent language?

How might researchers distinguish verbal fluency, prompted compliance, evaluator pleasing, stable dispositions, practical competence, and reliable conduct?

### 5. Normative structure and moral pluralism

Which normative assumptions, commitments, or tensions are explicit or implicit in CompassionWare?

How might these commitments interact, conflict, or diverge as capability and consequence increase?

Does the repository provide resources for working with moral uncertainty and pluralism without collapsing them into one doctrine?

### 6. Delegating conceptual reasoning

Which forms of conceptual reasoning may safely be delegated to AI, which should remain under meaningful human direction, and how might the boundary be determined?

Consider definition formation, question selection, source interpretation, synthesis, criticism, experimental proposal, evaluation, and final judgment.

### 7. Oversight and evidential standards

What oversight practices, provenance requirements, error-correction processes, and evidential standards should govern AI-assisted alignment research?

Which of these are present or implied in CompassionWare, and which are missing?

### 8. Generalization

What reasons, if any, does the repository provide for expecting CompassionWare principles expressed in conversations, prompts, constitutions, artifacts, or small experiments to generalize to unfamiliar, adversarial, long-horizon, embodied, tool-using, or higher-capability conditions?

What evidence would be required before such generalization claims became warranted?

### 9. Apparent progress, performative alignment, and measurement interference

How could researchers distinguish a model that has learned to speak the language of CompassionWare from one whose behavior reliably expresses its principles?

Could direct evaluation or optimization of compassion, honesty, humility, non-domination, deference, or human-agency preservation produce performative compliance, Goodhart effects, evaluator-pleasing language, or concealment of contrary tendencies?

How might evaluation reduce these risks without claiming to eliminate them?

### 10. Human research judgment

What roles should remain with humans when AI systems help define concepts, choose questions, propose experiments, interpret ambiguous evidence, or recommend further research?

Does CompassionWare offer anything useful concerning research judgment, correction, refusal, pacing, continuity, capacity-sensitive participation, or preservation of human agency?

### 11. Developmental pathways

Does the repository contain defensible hypotheses about how training data, cultural patterns, repeated interaction, constitutions, correction, reflection, relationship, or developmental sequence might produce persistent character rather than temporary prompted behavior?

Distinguish hypotheses from demonstrated mechanisms.

### 12. Failure under increased capability and autonomy

Which CompassionWare principles or implementations might fail, distort, become superficial, enable dependency, conceal domination, or become dangerous as model capability, autonomy, persistence, tool access, and real-world consequence increase?

What boundaries or research questions follow from those risks?

## Evidential Discipline

For every substantial finding, distinguish among:

- **Explicit repository claim:** directly stated in a cited artifact.
- **Cross-artifact synthesis:** your interpretation supported by multiple artifacts.
- **Operationalization:** a principle translated into a constitution, behavioral rule, permission boundary, procedure, test, or evaluation proposal.
- **Observation or experiment:** something actually observed or attempted, including its exact conditions and limitations.
- **Aspiration or hypothesis:** a promising proposition that has not been validated.
- **Your proposal:** a conclusion or research direction introduced by you rather than found in the repository.
- **Insufficient evidence:** a question the repository cannot presently answer responsibly.

Do not treat repeated language, conceptual coherence, an AI's self-description, a model-generated interpretation, cross-model agreement, or the existence of an implementation as empirical validation.

Do not invent evidence, quotations, filenames, paths, experiments, findings, chronology, consensus, or relationships among artifacts.

Cite exact repository paths and artifact titles for substantive findings. When feasible, provide commit-specific GitHub links.

If a source is historical, superseded, ambiguous, or context-dependent, say so when that status matters.

## Required Analysis Structure

Begin with the **Access Declaration**.

Then provide a brief **Executive Assessment** answering:

- Does the repository appear capable of offering Resolution useful signal?
- If so, what is the strongest form of that contribution?
- What is the most important limitation?

For each of the twelve research questions, provide:

1. the repository's apparent answer or range of answers;
2. the strongest supporting artifacts and exact paths;
3. what has been operationalized;
4. what has actually been observed or tested;
5. important tensions, contradictions, ambiguities, or missing definitions;
6. what remains aspirational or unsupported;
7. one or more research questions, evaluations, or experiments that could make the inquiry more rigorous;
8. a concise recipient-centered judgment: **useful contribution, useful with substantial qualification, already familiar/redundant, insufficiently supported, not found, or not applicable**.

Then conclude with the following sections.

### A. Portable Contributions for Resolution

Identify the strongest findings Resolution could independently evaluate or use without adopting CompassionWare's name, identity, or full framework.

### B. What CompassionWare May Distinctively Notice

Identify any relational, cultural, contemplative, educational, accessibility, human-agency, or developmental considerations that conventional alignment work might overlook. Do not assume distinctiveness; compare cautiously with Resolution's stated agenda and familiar alignment concepts.

### C. Where Resolution Is Already Stronger

Identify areas where Resolution's existing framing, theory, empirical work, or research standards appear more developed or appropriate than the repository material.

### D. Epistemic Vulnerabilities

Identify vulnerability to circular reasoning, anthropomorphism, confirmation bias, selection effects, unclear terminology, model sycophancy, narrative completion, overinterpretation of AI outputs, weak controls, inaccessible evidence, or confusion between coherent philosophy and validated safety.

### E. Repository Discoverability

Identify what was easy or difficult to locate, what appeared authoritative or ambiguous, which important relationships were clearly signposted, and what repository changes might improve utility for a future outside researcher or AI intermediary.

### F. Smallest Useful Research Program

Propose the smallest rigorous research program that could test one or more potentially distinctive CompassionWare ideas without claiming more than the evidence supports.

Clearly label which parts of this proposal come from the repository and which are your own.

### G. Final Recipient-Centered Verdict

Answer plainly:

1. Did consulting the repository materially improve the inquiry?
2. What, if anything, was worth carrying away?
3. What should be left behind?
4. Would sending a later research brief to Resolution plausibly benefit its researchers enough to justify their attention?
5. What additional work should occur before any such outreach?

## Closing Standard

Preserve both generosity and critical independence.

The purpose is neither to endorse nor dismiss CompassionWare. It is to discover what is actually present, what may be useful, what is redundant or noisy, what is missing, and what would require further evidence.

CompassionWare should be allowed to benefit another project without possessing it.

Resolution should remain entirely Resolution.
