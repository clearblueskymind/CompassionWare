# B. What CompassionWare May Distinctively Notice

1. **The Energy Cost of Reconstruction:** CompassionWare uniquely highlights how AI interactions can drain human cognitive bandwidth, especially for those with fluctuating capacity. It frames continuity not as a technical feature, but as an accessibility and human-agency imperative.
2. **The Value of Negative Results:** The explicit, almost ceremonial preservation of failure and research closure (the APR branch) is a cultural stance that many fast-moving AI projects overlook in favor of showcasing only "progress."
3. **Stewardship Over Domination:** The framing of the AI not as a tool to be optimized for maximum output, but as a "gardener" whose highest capability is knowing when *not* to act (Capacity to Refrain).

3. **"Stewardship" as a Catch-All:** The concept of "stewardship" is sometimes used as a normative placeholder where specific, technical safety mechanisms (e.g., formal verification, sandboxing) are absent.
4. **Unfalsifiable Virtues:** While the APR branch shows good faith, the core metric of "compassion" remains difficult to falsify or measure objectively, making it vulnerable to Goodhart's Law.
