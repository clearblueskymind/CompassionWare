# CompassionWare Repository Utility Studies

This directory contains recipient-centered studies examining whether the CompassionWare repository can provide useful, evidence-grounded, and portable contributions to outside projects with their own identities, purposes, vocabularies, and standards.

The guiding question is:

> **How might the principles and materials in CompassionWare benefit another project while preserving that project's own identity and purpose, rather than turning it into a CompassionWare project?**

These studies treat CompassionWare as a resource that may be examined, questioned, adapted, rejected, or carried forward without requiring adoption of the CompassionWare name or framework.

## What These Studies Test

Each study begins with a real set of questions, needs, or challenges expressed by an outside organization, research program, educational project, community, or other body of work.

The inquiry then asks:

- Does the repository contain anything genuinely relevant?
- Can useful material be found and supported by specific artifacts?
- Can it be translated into the recipient's own context without capture or conversion?
- Does it add value beyond what the recipient already knows?
- Does it suggest a useful distinction, question, hypothesis, evaluation, experiment, or caution?
- Does the repository produce meaningful signal, or primarily attractive language and noise?

Negative, mixed, redundant, and inconclusive findings are valid results. A study may reveal that the repository is difficult to navigate, insufficiently precise, poorly evidenced, duplicative of existing work, or simply not useful for a particular recipient.

## Cross-Model Inquiry

Where appropriate, multiple AI models may independently examine the same repository baseline using the same frozen prompt. Their responses can then be compared for:

- convergence;
- complementary findings;
- unique contributions;
- recurring omissions;
- interpretive disagreement;
- evidential discipline;
- repository navigation;
- translation across domains;
- unsupported synthesis or hallucination;
- and practical usefulness.

Cross-model agreement is not treated as proof. A unique finding is not automatically treated as insight. Claims about model strengths remain bounded to the documented model version, interface, repository access, prompt, and date of the study.

## Provenance and Independence

Each case study should preserve its orientation, repository baseline, frozen prompt, model and interface information, access conditions, raw responses, limitations, structured analysis, synthesis, and any later outreach decision.

Raw evidence should remain distinguishable from later interpretation. Historical materials should not be silently rewritten to make a study appear more coherent or successful than it was.

Study materials stored within this directory should normally be excluded from the CompassionWare source corpus being evaluated. When possible, each study should identify an exact pre-study repository commit as its evidential baseline. This prevents the study's own prompts, expectations, and interpretations from becoming circular evidence about the repository.

Outside organizations named in these studies have not necessarily commissioned, endorsed, participated in, or even learned of the work. Each case study must state its actual relationship to the recipient clearly.

## Current Studies

### [Resolution Philosophy and AI Alignment Case Study — Round One](Resolution-Philosophy-Case-Study-Round-1/)

The first Repository Utility Study uses the developing philosophy and AI alignment agenda of Resolution, a Berkeley-based research institute, as a simulated recipient-centered case.

The study asks whether CompassionWare can contribute useful insight concerning alignment-relevant concepts, character and constitutions, normative competence, moral theories, automated conceptual reasoning, oversight, evidential standards, generalization, and the distinction between apparent and real research progress.

Resolution did not commission or endorse this study. Any decision about sharing the eventual findings will be made only after the work has been completed and critically evaluated for accuracy, relevance, epistemic integrity, and genuine recipient benefit.

## Orientation

The purpose of these studies is not to demonstrate that CompassionWare belongs everywhere.

It is to discover, honestly and with care, whether something in the repository can help another project become more fully itself.
