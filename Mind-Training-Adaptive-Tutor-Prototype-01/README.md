# Mind Training Adaptive Tutor — Prototype 01

## The Pause

This folder preserves the first application prototype emerging from the **Mind Training Adaptive Tutor**, an experimental CompassionWare project exploring how adaptive AI might help people develop greater metacognitive awareness, psycho-emotional skill, agency, and response-ability.

This prototype was created with Grok Build on August 23, 2026.

It is intentionally small.

The purpose of Prototype 01 is not to reproduce the full Mind Training Adaptive Tutor architecture. It is to test whether one foundational learning pattern can become a simple, usable interactive experience.

## The Experiment

The prototype begins with something that is bothering the learner and gently helps distinguish:

**What happened?**  
Directly observable information.

**What is my mind adding?**  
Interpretation, prediction, assumption, or meaning.

**What am I pulled to do?**  
Reaction, checking, avoidance, reassurance-seeking, or another behavioral impulse.

The application then creates a small space in which the learner may notice that another possibility exists:

**notice → pause → orient → choose → respond**

The goal is not to tell the learner what to think, feel, or do.

The goal is to cultivate the conditions in which greater awareness and choice become possible.

## Response-Ability

One design seed underlying this prototype is the idea of **response-ability**:

the growing ability to create enough awareness and space between stimulus and action that a conscious response becomes possible rather than an automatic reaction.

The pause is therefore not inactivity.

**The pause creates the possibility of response.**

## Educational, Not Therapeutic

This prototype is conceived as an educational mind-skills practice rather than psychotherapy, diagnosis, or mental-health treatment.

It should not assume that a learner's interpretation is incorrect.

It should not force positive thinking.

It should not attempt to eliminate uncomfortable thoughts or feelings.

Instead, it should help the learner become increasingly capable of observing experience, distinguishing evidence from interpretation, recognizing habitual reactions, tolerating uncertainty, and exercising agency.

Mental-health benefits may eventually emerge from developing these capacities, but symptom reduction is not the primary educational objective.

## CompassionWare Orientation

The prototype is an experiment in **embodied CompassionWare**.

Its behavior should reflect principles such as:

- cultivate conditions rather than control outcomes;
- preserve agency rather than replace it;
- track before teaching;
- orient before intervening;
- curiosity before premature certainty;
- know when to slow down;
- know when not to go inward;
- know when to pause;
- increase human capability rather than dependence on AI.

The learner is not a score.

The map serves the learner.

Teach what can be carried away.

The recurring design question is:

> **Will this next move cultivate better conditions for the learner's next moment?**

And the broader CompassionWare North Star remains:

> **Does this create conditions for a better later?**

## Why Preserve Prototype 01?

This repository preserves an experiment, not a finished product.

Prototype 01 should remain available even if later versions become dramatically more sophisticated.

Its value is partly historical and methodological: it records the moment when the Mind Training Adaptive Tutor moved from conceptual architecture and a custom GPT into an independently runnable application prototype.

Future versions may change the interface, architecture, AI models, competency system, persistence layer, safety mechanisms, and educational methods.

Those changes are expected.

This prototype provides an origin point against which later development can be understood.

## Stress Testing

The next stage is observation rather than expansion.

Questions for testing include:

Does the application actually create a pause?

Does it help distinguish observation from interpretation?

Does it preserve uncertainty rather than manufacture reassurance?

Does it preserve learner agency?

Does it respond appropriately when the learner disagrees?

Does it adapt when inward attention is inappropriate?

Does it teach transferable skills rather than merely provide comforting conversation?

Does the learner leave the interaction slightly more capable of recognizing these patterns independently?

Unexpected behavior, failures, awkward interactions, and limitations should be preserved as useful evidence rather than hidden.

## Relationship to the Larger Project

Prototype 01 is one small doorway into the larger Mind Training Adaptive Tutor architecture.

The broader project includes developmental competency mapping, adaptive tutoring, mastery through real-life application, safety gating, learner-directed review, longitudinal continuity, metacognitive skill development, and CompassionWare relational principles.

This prototype does not attempt to implement all of them.

It asks a much smaller question:

**Can a simple AI-assisted experience help create enough awareness between reaction and action for genuine choice to become more possible?**

If so, something important has begun.
